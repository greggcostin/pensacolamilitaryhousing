GET TO KNOW THE EVERYDAY PLACES

Put a few real places on your map.

Gulf Islands National Seashore

Johnson Beach

Beach crossovers, picnic pavilions, restrooms and an accessible boardwalk trail make this a useful place to experience the eastern end of the island. An entrance fee applies, and the National Park Service warns that parking can fill during busy seasons.

National Park Service: Perdido Key Area

Bring this question: How will you reach the beach on an ordinary weekend, including parking and carrying your gear?

Beach, dunes and boardwalks

Perdido Key State Park

The state park pairs Gulf beach access with dune-protecting boardwalks and covered picnic tables. It is a different access option from Johnson Beach, with its own entrance arrangements.

Florida State Parks: Perdido Key

Bring this question: Does the property include documented beach access, or will you use a public park?

A mainland waterfront option

Big Lagoon State Park

Big Lagoon offers a boat launch connecting to the Intracoastal Waterway. Its lagoon setting gives you a different way to spend time on the water from an open-Gulf beach day.

Florida State Parks: Big Lagoon experiences

Bring this question: For boating, does a nearby launch work for you, or is a documented private slip essential?

A specific stop on your shortlist

Lost Key Golf Club

Lost Key's official site describes a public, 18-hole Arnold Palmer golf course. If you are considering a nearby home, confirm separately which amenities, memberships and fees actually belong to that property.

Lost Key Golf Club: official course information

Bring this question: Which amenities convey with ownership, and which require separate payment or access?

THE COMPLETE OWNERSHIP PICTURE

What does it cost beyond the asking price?

Start with the property.

  • Compare current listings with recent closed sales for the same property type, building and location.
  • Have your lender estimate principal, interest and closing costs for the actual financing scenario.
  • Ask how taxes would be assessed for your ownership; the seller's current bill may not reflect your future bill.

Price the exposure.

  • Obtain property-specific homeowners or condo-unit coverage and flood quotes as applicable.
  • Read the coverage, exclusions and deductibles, including what the association's master policy leaves to the unit owner.
  • Allow for utilities, maintenance and a reserve for repairs.

Read the shared obligations.

  • Request the association's budget, reserve information, current dues and known or proposed assessments.
  • Ask for the inspection and reserve-study reports applicable to that building.
  • Confirm use restrictions and the lender's project requirements before relying on a financing plan.

Source notes: standard homeowners policies generally do not cover flooding (FloodSmart: flood insurance basics). Building and reserve-study questions should be checked against the association's actual records and Florida DBPR: condominium questions and reserve studies.

CLEAR ANSWERS BEFORE THE NEXT STEP

Perdido Key questions, answered.

How should I compare prices for Perdido Key homes and condos?

Compare active listings with recent closed sales for the same property type, building or location. Gulf frontage, deeded access, condition, association costs and assessments can change the comparison. A ZIP-wide value index is not a price for a specific Perdido Key property; request a current, property-specific comparison.

Does a Perdido Key address include private beach access?

Do not assume that it does. Check the deed, survey, association documents and any recorded access agreement. Public options include Perdido Key State Park and the National Park Service's Johnson Beach area, each with its own access and parking arrangements.

What should I review before buying a Perdido Key condo?

Ask for the association budget, reserve information, applicable inspection and reserve-study reports, current and proposed assessments, insurance details and use restrictions. Confirm with your lender whether the project fits your financing. Compare those findings with the unit's inspection and your complete monthly ownership budget.

Which schools should I research near Perdido Key?

Hellen Caro Elementary, Jim C. Bailey Middle and Escambia High are useful starting points in our school directory. Their 2025-26 Florida accountability grades are B, B and C respectively. Confirm attendance for the exact address with the district, and use the School Finder to explore other public, private and Christian options.

Can Gregg help compare Perdido Key with Orange Beach or Gulf Shores?

Yes. Gregg Costin is licensed in Florida and Alabama. The same team can help you compare properties across the state line, while checking the applicable location, ownership documents, taxes, insurance and use restrictions for each home.

How should I compare Pensacola-area homes for a military move?

Start with your actual NAS Pensacola, Corry Station or other reporting destination, then compare the complete housing payment and property condition. Gregg Costin connects the military move plan with civilian neighborhood guides so you can evaluate the house, documents and route together.

Check the property's school assignment through the district, obtain a property-specific insurance quote and compare your route at the times you expect to travel. Gulf Breeze, Perdido Key and central Pensacola have different bridge and road connections.
