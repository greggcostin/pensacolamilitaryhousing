A military move creates several different residence questions at once: where the family will live, what the orders require, where legal residence remains and how the property is treated for tax purposes. A VA loan approval or a Florida mailing address does not answer all of them.

Start by identifying the tax year and the property you want reviewed. Then separate a new homestead application from maintaining an existing exemption while away on service obligations. This guide focuses on that distinction so a household can make a sound purchase or rental decision without assuming the paperwork takes care of itself.

How large is Florida's additional homestead exemption in 2026?

Florida's additional homestead exemption is up to $26,411 for 2026 and applies to non-school taxes. The DOR inflation table establishes the annual amount. The actual savings depend on qualifying value, eligibility and the parcel's taxing authorities.

The first homestead tier covers up to $25,000 of qualifying value for all taxing authorities. The additional tier applies above the relevant assessed-value threshold. Santa Rosa's exemption explanation sets out those tiers. Do not multiply the combined reduction by an undifferentiated tax rate that includes schools.

Does the 2026 assessment cap limit the whole tax bill?

The 2026 Save Our Homes assessment increase limit is 2.7% for qualifying continuing homesteads. The county assessment explanation distinguishes value assessment from the final bill. Changes in rates, assessments, ownership or improvements can produce a different tax result.

The seller's protected assessment can be a poor estimate for a new buyer. Ask the property appraiser for the buyer-based values and possible exemptions. Use that information in the household housing-budget worksheet, alongside actual income, debts and insurance quotes.

What does the exemption mean in dollars?

Calculate each applicable portion separately. This hypothetical example assumes enough qualifying assessed value to use both tiers, a 7-mill school rate and a 10-mill non-school rate. Those rates are invented for illustration and do not represent a Pensacola, Gulf Breeze or Milton parcel.

For a buyer in Gulf Breeze, verify whether the parcel is inside the city or elsewhere in Santa Rosa County before using a tax estimate. For a Milton owner leaving for a new assignment, document whether the existing exemption will remain qualified before comparing rental cash flow with a sale.

Can active-duty service preserve an existing homestead while renting?

A military exception can protect a qualifying homestead from the ordinary rental-abandonment rule. It does not create an exemption for every investment property owned by a service member. Review Section 196.061 and the county's requirements before relying on continued treatment.

The DOR military benefits brochure describes the exception for property already owned and used as homestead. Ask the county how your orders, prior use, legal residence and proposed rental arrangement fit. Notify the office when service status or other material facts change.

Santa Rosa requires Florida residents away on active duty to renew annually with current orders, residency evidence and an LES with income redacted. Follow the county renewal instructions. Keep the confirmation with your property records; assume neither an automatic renewal nor permanent eligibility after separation.

Does permission to keep homestead also permit a rental business?

No single tax determination establishes the complete rental plan. Separately check the loan and occupancy obligations, insurance for the actual use, association restrictions and jurisdiction requirements. A favorable tax answer can coexist with a rental restriction or an insurance policy that needs changing.

Use the PCS sale-versus-rental guide to compare documented income with vacancy, management, repairs, taxes, insurance, dues, debt service and reserves. Test a delay in finding a tenant and a repair during the same period. Do not make continued exemption or uninterrupted rent a hidden assumption.

Furnished medium-term or vacation use deserves its own review. A longer advertised stay does not by itself establish permission under every association or local rule. Describe the intended occupancy accurately to the insurer and lender, then obtain the address-specific requirements before buying furniture or advertising availability.

How should a new applicant handle the filing calendar?

Identify ownership and permanent-residence status for the applicable assessment date, then obtain the county's current filing instructions. A purchase late in the year should prompt planning for the next tax year; it should not trigger an assumption that the seller's exemption transfers with the deed.

  1. Find the parcel in the correct county record.
  2. Identify the first tax year for which you may qualify.
  3. Gather the county's required ownership and residence evidence.
  4. Disclose any residency-based benefit claimed elsewhere.
  5. Submit through the official channel and keep a receipt.
  6. Review the determination, notice and bill when issued.

Start with Escambia's current calendar, Santa Rosa's linked instructions or the Okaloosa filing portal. As this September review occurs after ordinary spring filing, contact the office promptly about the relevant year and any available late procedure. Do not infer an extension.

What other military benefits should you ask about?

Disability and qualifying deployment benefits have separate conditions and evidence requirements. A deployment does not automatically match a designated operation, and a disability payment percentage does not automatically establish a full homestead exemption. Ask the property appraiser to identify the applicable category before adding it to the budget.

The veteran county filing guide and disabled veteran benefits overview can help organize that discussion. Keep the exemption question separate from VA loan eligibility; different agencies decide different benefits using different records.

What should you check after the county responds?

Verify the exemption category and tax year on the property record, and keep any annual renewal requirement on the household calendar. Send approved changes to the mortgage servicer when escrow is involved and review its analysis. Do not reduce a required loan payment on your own.

Continue budgeting the costs homestead does not answer: insurance, repairs, utilities and possible association charges. Use the military insurance guide to prepare quotes for a move or change in use. A tax benefit is useful when it sits inside an accurate ownership plan.
