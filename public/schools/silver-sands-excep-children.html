A school plan for your PCS

Moving with a student: Silver Sands-Excep. Children

Considering Silver Sands-Excep. Children in Fort Walton Beach during a military move? Pair the school's academic information with a plan for admission, records and the first week.

Confirm enrollment in Okaloosa County

The Okaloosa registration and school locator distinguish address-based assignment from controlled open enrollment. Check which applies here, including any program application. Bring your projected arrival date to the registrar rather than assuming an available map result means an available seat. Ask the liaison whether your transfer qualifies for Interstate Compact provisions and how to resolve a records or placement issue.

Carry the learning history forward

Request a portable copy of report cards, transcripts, current coursework and relevant learning plans before you leave. If your child has an IEP, 504 plan or other support arrangement, arrange a handoff with the receiving staff and ask what support will be available at the start. Use the Military OneSource school-transition guide to prepare for that conversation.

Match the handoff to the student

For a family entering different grades at Silver Sands-Excep. Children, request a separate placement conversation for each child. A teen needs a review of transferred credits, graduation requirements and course sequencing; a younger sibling needs a classroom transition plan. Confirm which grades share a campus, schedules and arrival procedures instead of assuming one arrangement serves every student.

Connect school, home and duty station

The Okaloosa military-family resources identify Eglin's liaison for Eglin, Duke Field and 7th Special Forces Group families. Hurlburt has its own liaison; use the contact matching your assignment. Local contacts: Hurlburt Field School Liaison and Eglin School Liaison Office. Ask about youth introductions, deployment-related support and coordination with school staff. Availability of a particular service at Silver Sands-Excep. Children should be confirmed directly.

Check the school-day route to your actual duty gate, dismissal time and backup pickup plan. The map estimates travel; it does not establish a bus route, attendance boundary or guaranteed commute.
