## The Four Priority Profiles

### 1. AHRN.com — Automated Housing Referral Network

- **Where:** https://www.ahrn.com/agent-signup (or contact AHRN agent services if signup is gated)
- **Why:** #1 military rental + agent directory after MilitaryByOwner. DoD-affiliated.
- **What to fill:** Canonical NAP + bio + headshot. Select all 7 bases as service areas.
- **Likely vetting:** May require proof of license — have your FL/AL license cards handy.
- **Time:** 15–20 min

### 2. MilitaryByOwner (MHO)

- **Where:** https://www.militarybyowner.com/agent-program
- **Why:** Largest military-housing classified site. Agent directory ranks high for "military Realtor [city]" queries. Their blog accepts guest posts.
- **What to fill:** Canonical NAP + bio + headshot + cover photo (consider one of your military-era photos from the main site).
- **Specialty checkboxes:** VA Loan · Military Relocation · PCS
- **Bonus action:** Once your profile is up, pitch the MHO blog editor a guest post — the May 2026 PPA piece you already have on `/blog` is a great fit. Their blog is real domain authority that links back to you.
- **Time:** 15–20 min profile + 30 min if you submit a guest post

### 3. PCSgrades.com

- **Where:** https://www.pcsgrades.com/agents (look for "Become a Recommended Agent" or contact form)
- **Why:** Review-aggregator military families use to vet agents. You can transplant your Google review momentum here.
- **What to fill:** Canonical NAP + bio + headshot.
- **Vetting:** They phone-screen agents — be ready to talk about how you serve military families and what credentials you hold.
- **Bonus action:** Ask your last 5 closed military clients to leave a PCSgrades review (in addition to Google/Zillow). Your `reviews.greggcostin.com` page can be updated to add a PCSgrades card once you're listed.
- **Time:** 20–30 min profile + phone screen

### 4. MyBaseGuide.com

- **Where:** https://www.mybaseguide.com/business-listing-signup
- **Why:** Base-info portal military families check before PCS. Agent directory rides alongside base content — high-relevance traffic.
- **What to fill:** Canonical NAP + bio + headshot. Submit ONE listing per base if their system allows (more bases = more surface).
- **Time:** 20–30 min for all 7 base submissions
