YOUR PART OF THE COAST

Choose the block as carefully as the house.

The city identifies separate Pensacola, Palafox, North Hill and Old East Hill historic districts. Historic character and historic-district regulation are related but different questions. A property outside a preservation district can still require permits, while a property inside one may have additional design review. Start with the actual parcel and the city guidance rather than an architectural style in a listing.

CLEAR ANSWERS BEFORE THE NEXT STEP

East Hill & Downtown Pensacola questions, answered.

Is all of East Hill a historic preservation district?

No. The City of Pensacola maps Old East Hill as a specific preservation district. Verify the actual parcel and applicable rules instead of assuming the broader neighborhood name determines them.

What deserves extra attention on an older home?

Review the structure, roof, drainage, electrical and plumbing systems, renovation permits and insurability. Age or appearance alone cannot establish condition.

How do I compare a downtown condo with a detached home?

Use the complete monthly and upfront costs, parking and storage, repair responsibilities and intended use. For a condo, examine association documents alongside the unit inspection.

How should I compare Pensacola-area homes for a military move?

Start with your actual NAS Pensacola, Corry Station or other reporting destination, then compare the complete housing payment and property condition. Gregg Costin connects the military move plan with civilian neighborhood guides so you can evaluate the house, documents and route together.

Check the property's school assignment through the district, obtain a property-specific insurance quote and compare your route at the times you expect to travel. Gulf Breeze, Perdido Key and central Pensacola have different bridge and road connections.
