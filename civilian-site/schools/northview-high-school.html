Get to know this school

A closer look at Northview High School.

Northview High describes career academies that combine academic classes with a chosen career pathway. Its AgriTech page details a sequence beginning with Agriscience Foundations and continuing into agritechnology.

The 2025–26 Florida DOE report classifies Northview High School in the high category, as a district public school in Escambia County. Its accountability grade is C; it earned 57% of possible accountability points. The published grade sequence is C in 2023–24, B in 2024–25, and C in 2025–26.

The school in perspectiveEditorial guide · 2026-09-06

Agriculture connects science, mechanical work and business skills

Our interpretation of published school information, not a campus inspection or an additional official rating.

Northview's AgriTech sequence is more varied than its name alone suggests. The published progression moves from scientific foundations into plant and animal topics, tools and recordkeeping, then into applications such as welding, small-engine work, irrigation repair and financial management. For a student who likes understanding how things work and seeing a practical result, that combination gives the academic coursework a tangible context. It also creates several interests to explore before narrowing a career direction.

FFA adds a related leadership dimension through activities such as speaking, running meetings and taking organizational responsibility. The meaningful question is how a student's classroom work and participation connect over several years. A family considering a transfer should bring the existing transcript and map where the student can enter the sequence. A compelling project in an upper-level course is useful only if the prerequisites and remaining high-school schedule allow the student to reach it.

Why it may fit

Worth examining for students interested in applied science, agriculture, mechanical problem-solving or the business side of practical work, with opportunities to develop speaking and leadership skills.

What to weigh

The sequence requires planning. A late start, transferred credits or competing electives may affect access to advanced courses; current projects and FFA activities should be confirmed with instructors.

A useful local detail

Northview's official address places the campus on West Highway 4 in Bratt. Some directories use Century, so use the school's own address when planning an appointment.

One question worth asking

Ask to trace one current project from the underlying science through hands-on work and recordkeeping, then identify which course teaches each part.

Understand the results

How the official results compare.

Its 57% overall points result is 2 percentage points below the 59% median among 7 other high schools in Escambia County with reported data.

What the results suggest.

Look beyond the latest letter

The sequence C → B → C is a reason to ask what changed between the three reporting years. A letter-grade change is not itself a measure of an individual child's learning growth. High and combination schools also faced higher grading thresholds in 2025–26, so a lower letter alone is not proof of weaker instruction.

A result worth noticing

Social studies is 74%, compared with a 72% median for 7 other local schools in the same state category. That 2-point difference is the largest positive peer gap among the subject measures available here. Ask which learning opportunities sit behind it and how a new student would access them.

Where to look closer

The English language arts result is 48%, 6 percentage points below its 54% local peer median. This is a specific area to discuss, rather than a verdict on the whole school. Ask how teachers identify a student who needs help, what support looks like during the week, and how progress is shared with families.

Finishing school and taking the next step

The published graduation rate is 95%, while college and career acceleration is 84%. Both refer to the state's 2024–25 cohort measures, but they answer different questions. Acceleration reflects specified qualifying accomplishments; it is not a college-admission rate or a count of every student taking an advanced class. Ask which pathways fit your child's goals and what the entry requirements are.

What should actually matter to parents

A letter grade is one data point, not a verdict. When families ask us how to evaluate a school, we point them to the same checklist every time:

  • Visit in person. Ten minutes in the front office and hallways tells you more about culture than any statistic.
  • Match the school to YOUR child. A school that is right for a self-directed kid may be wrong for one who needs structure, and the reverse. Programs (arts, STEM, athletics, IB/AP, ESE services) matter more than a one-letter summary.
  • Look at the trend, not one year. A school moving from C to B to A is a different story than one drifting the other way. We show three years on every school page for exactly this reason.
  • Read the components. Two schools with the same letter can have very different achievement profiles. The subject-area percentages show where a school is strong.
  • Confirm zoning before you buy. Attendance zones change. Verify the address you are considering with the district's current zoning tools, not a listing's claim.
  • Context matters. Research consistently shows family engagement outweighs most school-level differences. The percentages here measure the school; they do not predict your child.

Verify and dig deeper

  • Escambia County School District: enrollment, attendance zoning, school choice programs, and school contact pages.
  • Florida EduData portal: the state's full report card for every public school (search by school name).
  • All Pensacola-area school reports on this site, and the neighborhood guide for where each community sits.
