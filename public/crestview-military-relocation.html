Crestview Military Relocation — The FL023 Value Play

Crestview draws the same Fort Walton Beach–MHA BAH as Niceville and the coast — at a median price well over $100,000 less. Here’s the full math: 7th Group and Duke Field commutes, builder price bands, school grades, and the inland insurance advantage nobody else quantifies.

Why Crestview Is the FL023 Value Play

Every duty station in Okaloosa County — Eglin AFB, Hurlburt Field, Duke Field — pays BAH from the same table: the Fort Walton Beach MHA, FL023. Your allowance doesn't change whether you buy in Niceville, on the coast, or 25 miles north in Crestview. The price of the house changes enormously.

As of mid-2026, Movoto puts Crestview's median sold price at $334,900 (July 2026), and Zillow's average home value sits at $300,660, up just 0.3% year over year. Niceville and coastal Okaloosa typically run $450K+. Same BAH check, well over $100,000 less house price at the median — that is the entire Crestview thesis, and it's why the town has quietly become the default landing zone for 7th Special Forces Group families and a growing share of Eglin commuters. If you're weighing the two directly, I wrote a full Niceville vs. Crestview comparison; this page is the deep Crestview brief.

7th SFG, Camp Bull Simons, and the Real Commute Map

Why the 7th Group concentrated in Crestview

Camp "Bull" Simons — the 7th Special Forces Group (Airborne) compound — sits on the Eglin reservation at the 77th Special Forces Way interchange on SR 85, adjacent to Duke Field. Local news routinely describes it as being "in Crestview," roughly 45 minutes north of the main Eglin base. Read that again: the 7th Group's duty location is closer to Crestview than to Eglin proper. For a soldier assigned to Bull Simons, Niceville and Fort Walton Beach aren't just more expensive — they're a longer drive. Crestview is the shortest commute in the county, and the neighborhoods reflect it.

Commute times that hold up

  • Duke Field / Camp Bull Simons: ~20-25 minutes straight down SR 85. This is the commute that makes Crestview work. Full base rundown: Duke Field guide, and if you're weighing privatized housing against buying, see on-base vs. off-base at Duke Field.
  • Eglin AFB main base: ~30-45 minutes via SR 85 south; the SR 123 bypass skirts Niceville. Crestview commuters generally enter on the east side — the East Gate sits on the Valparaiso side off SR 397. The visitor center there has been listed at weekdays 6 a.m.-2 p.m., but that listing may predate 2026 — verify current gate and VCC hours at eglin.af.mil before you drive. My Eglin AFB base guide covers the gate layout in detail.
  • Hurlburt Field: ~55-65 minutes. I'll be blunt: Crestview is not a Hurlburt play. If your orders read Hurlburt, look at Mary Esther, Fort Walton Beach, or Navarre instead.

Peak traffic on SR 85 adds 5-10+ minutes to all of these — everyone in Crestview funnels down the same corridor at the same hours. That's the honest trade for the price advantage, and where you buy inside Crestview shapes it: the south-side subdivisions off SR 85 shave real minutes off a Duke Field or Eglin drive versus the far north end.

The Inland Money Advantage: Insurance, Flood, and Taxes

This is Crestview's most underrated argument, and the one no competing guide quantifies. Florida homeowners insurance is priced heavily on wind exposure, and Crestview is 25+ miles inland.

  • Homeowners insurance: Crestview policies typically run $1,800-$3,500/year, against a county-wide average of roughly $2,400-$3,800 for a $300K dwelling — the inland location earns a lower wind-exposure rating than coastal Okaloosa. Hurricane/wind deductibles still run 2-5% of dwelling coverage, and a wind-mitigation inspection can cut premiums 20-45% — on a new build with current code features, always order one.
  • Flood: most of Crestview sits in FEMA Zone X — minimal hazard, flood insurance optional rather than lender-required. Owners who carry it anyway pay roughly $400-$2,000/year. Compare that to the coastal AE/VE zones in Navarre, Gulf Breeze, or Destin, where flood coverage is mandatory and priced accordingly.
  • Property taxes: Okaloosa County held its millage flat at 3.8308 for the ninth straight year in the FY2026 budget. The county's median effective rate is about 0.90%, median annual bill $2,331. Addresses inside Crestview city limits pay a city millage on top of that — pull the exact total for your parcel from the Okaloosa County Property Appraiser before you rely on a payment estimate. Florida's homestead exemption and VA-disability property-tax exemptions can shrink that bill further once you're in the house.

Stack those three lines and the monthly gap between Crestview and the coast is wider than the sticker prices alone suggest. When I run buyer numbers, insurance is routinely the line item that surprises coastal shoppers — and the one that flatters Crestview.

The Investor Angle: BAH-Backed Tenant Demand

Crestview is also the county's cleanest rental math, which matters twice: once if you're buying investment property outright, and again in three years when your next set of orders arrives and you're deciding whether to rent or sell on your way out.

  • Rents: Zumper's median across all beds and types was $1,850 (Feb 2026); Zillow Rental Manager shows a median of $2,195 (range $850-$3,200); the average house rents for about $2,041/month. Apartments average $1,474 (RentCafe, down 3.4% YoY) — the strength is in houses, not units.
  • Product mix: 51% of Crestview rentals are single-family homes, with only ~155 houses listed for rent at a time — thin supply against a steady stream of incoming FL023 families.
  • Tenant demand floor: every E-5 with dependents assigned to Eglin, Hurlburt, or Duke Field carries $2,433/month in BAH; even junior enlisted with dependents carry $2,340. A $2,000 single-family rent sits comfortably under that. Against a $270K Day's Landing entry price, $2,000/month is roughly 0.7% monthly rent-to-price — cash-flow math coastal Okaloosa simply can't match at today's prices.

The 7th Group presence adds a structural tailwind: Bull Simons soldiers rotate in and out on a cycle, and Crestview is their shortest commute — your tenant pool replenishes itself. Frequently Asked Questions

Why do 7th Special Forces Group families choose Crestview?

Camp "Bull" Simons, the 7th SFG compound, sits on the Eglin reservation at the 77th Special Forces Way interchange on SR 85 — adjacent to Duke Field and closer to Crestview than to the main Eglin base. For soldiers assigned there, Crestview is the shortest commute in Okaloosa County, roughly 20-25 minutes down SR 85. See the Duke Field base guide at /bases/duke-field for the full picture.

How long is the commute from Crestview to Eglin AFB?

Plan on roughly 30-45 minutes south on SR 85, with the SR 123 bypass skirting Niceville; peak traffic adds 5-10+ minutes. Crestview commuters typically enter on the east side — the East Gate is on the Valparaiso side off SR 397. Details in my Eglin AFB base guide.

What is BAH in Crestview, FL?

Crestview falls in the Fort Walton Beach MHA (FL023), which covers Eglin, Hurlburt Field, and Duke Field. 2026 with-dependents rates: E-5 $2,433, E-7 $2,841, O-3 $3,399 per month. Run your exact rank on my BAH calculator to convert it into a purchase budget.

What do homes cost in Crestview?

As of mid-2026, Movoto shows a median sold price of $334,900 (July 2026) while Zillow's average home value sits at $300,660 — call it roughly $300-335K depending on the source and month. Niceville and coastal Okaloosa typically run $450K+, which is the entire Crestview argument in one line.

What new-construction communities are in Crestview?

NewHomeSource counts roughly 12 builders and 35 new communities in the Crestview area. Standouts with published pricing: D.R. How are the schools in Crestview?

Okaloosa County School District earned a district-wide A for 2024-25, with 92% of its schools graded A or B. In Crestview zones, Antioch Elementary, Walker Elementary, Davidson Middle, and Baker School all earned A grades; Northwood Elementary earned a B, one point shy of an A. Crestview High carries a B+ on Niche — verify its current state letter grade at fldoe.org before you lock in a zone.

Do I need flood insurance in Crestview?

Usually not — most of Crestview sits in FEMA Zone X, minimal flood hazard, so lenders don't require flood coverage the way they do in coastal AE/VE zones. Owners who buy it anyway pay roughly $400-$2,000 per year depending on zone and elevation. That's a real monthly-payment advantage over Navarre, Gulf Breeze, or Destin — see my Florida home insurance guide at /florida-home-insurance-military.

Is Crestview a good rental investment when I PCS out?

The math is among the best in the county: median rents run $1,850 (Zumper, Feb 2026) to $2,195 (Zillow Rental Manager), 51% of Crestview rentals are single-family homes, and FL023 BAH gives every E-5-and-up tenant $2,433+ with dependents to spend. Honestly, no — plan on 55-65 minutes each way. Crestview is a Duke Field, 7th SFG, and Eglin play. For Hurlburt, look at Mary Esther, Fort Walton Beach, or Navarre instead; my Hurlburt Field base guide breaks down the commute zones.
