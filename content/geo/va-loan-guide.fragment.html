What does zero down leave for me to pay?

With sufficient entitlement, lender approval and an appraisal that supports the transaction, a VA purchase may require no down payment. VA purchase loans do not require monthly mortgage insurance. Zero down still leaves a cash-to-close question: lender and settlement charges, prepaid taxes and insurance, deposits, inspections and any appraisal gap or other required down payment. Does an existing VA loan stop my next purchase?

Full entitlement removes the VA county loan limit for an eligible buyer. It does not mean unlimited lender approval. If entitlement is already charged to another loan, the lender must calculate the remaining amount using the current COE and the new property's county limit. The down-payment question depends on that calculation, rather than simply whether you have used VA before. VA's entitlement and remaining-entitlement method.

VA lists restoration routes that include selling the prior home and paying off its VA loan, a qualified veteran assuming the loan and substituting entitlement, and a one-time restoration after paying the prior VA loan in full while keeping the property. A release of liability and a restoration of entitlement are different issues. Keeping a home with an active VA loan calls for a remaining-entitlement review. VA restoration requirements.

Can I buy a condo in Destin, Perdido Key or Gulf Shores?

VA permits a qualifying purchase in a VA-approved condominium project. The project approval, your loan approval and the unit's condition are separate matters. Have the lender verify the exact project before spending money based on an assumption that every unit in a beach market can use VA financing. The home also needs qualifying personal occupancy; a purchase solely for vacation-rental use does not meet that requirement. VA eligible property types.

How should I structure an offer with seller credits?

VA separates normal buyer closing costs from seller concessions. Its concession limit is 4% of the property's reasonable value, with ordinary closing-cost credits treated separately. Ask the lender to classify each proposed credit, including any funding-fee payment or debt payoff, before putting the structure into the offer. Avoid treating 4% of the contract price as a universal limit on everything the seller can pay. VA's explanation of seller contributions.

What happens if the appraisal or inspection raises a problem?

A VA appraisal addresses value and minimum property requirements. It does not replace an independent home inspection. VA recommends an inspection and a contract with the VA escape clause or option clause. Discuss the inspection contingencies you need with your agent before signing. If value is below the contract price, options may include a supported reconsideration of value, a price negotiation, paying the difference or exercising applicable contract rights. How do we coordinate a remote purchase with military orders?

  1. Start with the reporting destination. Confirm the unit and address with your sponsor. Eglin Main, Duke Field, Camp Bull Simons and Hurlburt are different trips; NAS Pensacola and Corry are separate locations.
  2. Complete lender review early. Discuss your income after the move, entitlement, occupancy, debts and expected cash to close. Send financial documents through the lender's secure channel.
  3. Evaluate the property independently. Use video tours, inspections, an insurance quote and association or title documents. Record what you could not verify remotely.
  4. Align the contract and arrival plan. Confirm closing, possession, inspection deadlines, temporary lodging and signing options with the people responsible for each step.
  5. Prepare a backup. A report date, appraisal delay or repair issue can change the schedule. Keep lodging and financing contingencies in the plan.
