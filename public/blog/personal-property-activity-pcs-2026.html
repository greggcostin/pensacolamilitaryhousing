What the New Personal Property Activity (PPA) Means for Your 2026 PCS to Pensacola

On May 1, 2026, the Pentagon stood up the Personal Property Activity to run military moves after the HomeSafe household goods contract collapsed. Here is what changed, what it means for your move to NAS Pensacola or Eglin, and how it affects your housing timeline.

If you have orders to the Florida Panhandle this year, whether to NAS Pensacola or Eglin AFB, the way your household goods get moved looks different than it did 18 months ago. On May 1, 2026, the Pentagon officially stood up the Personal Property Activity (PPA), a permanent agency that now runs PCS household goods moves for every service member.

I updated this post on August 12, 2026 with what has happened since the stand-up: the new booking flow, the 2026 PPM payout rules, weight entitlements, the extended claims window, and the part I care about most as your Realtor, how all of it should shape your housing timeline.

Here is the plain-English version of what happened, and more importantly, what it means for your move to Pensacola.

What Is the Personal Property Activity, and Why Did the Pentagon Create It?

The Personal Property Activity is the permanent agency that took over military household goods moves on May 1, 2026. It is headquartered at Scott Air Force Base, Illinois, reports directly to the Secretary of Defense, and Army Maj. Gen. Lance Curtis is its first commander. It replaced the failed single-contractor HomeSafe experiment with a centrally managed version of the traditional moving system most of us already know.

The backstory matters because it explains the system you are booking into. For years the Pentagon tried to privatize PCS moves under a single company. That effort was the Global Household Goods Contract (GHC), awarded to HomeSafe Alliance: one contractor, one app, one point of accountability. It did not go well.

As HomeSafe took on real volume in 2024 and early 2025, families reported missed pickups, belongings delivered weeks late, and constant problems with the HomeSafe Connect platform. By spring 2025 the Army had paused its moves through HomeSafe, and on June 18, 2025, the Pentagon terminated the GHC contract for cause.

To stabilize the system, the Pentagon stood up a PCS Joint Task Force in mid-2025 under Maj. Gen. Curtis. In January 2026, the Secretary of Defense signed the implementation memorandum directing that task force to become permanent, and the Personal Property Activity officially launched on May 1, 2026.

The organization runs with roughly 120 personnel and a joint operations center that consolidates move data across all the services, and it is replacing the Defense Personal Property System software, which is more than 25 years old, with commercial alternatives (Military.com, 2026).

How Do You Actually Book a Household Goods Move in 2026?

Through your installation's transportation office (the personal property office), the same as before the HomeSafe era. The PPA's guidance is blunt: contact the office as soon as you have orders in hand, even a soft copy. The new official site at ppa.mil, launched May 1, 2026, consolidates counseling, scheduling, PPM, and claims guidance in one place, and the 833-MIL-MOVE line (833-645-6683) runs 24/7 through September 15 during peak season.

The system is busy. Through June 30, 2026, the program had completed 138,570 household goods pickups, ahead of the 134,282 at the same point in 2025, out of roughly 300,000 military and civilian household moves the government handles each year (Army Times, July 1, 2026).

Capacity is the choke point every summer, and the PPA knows it: it just ran an open season from July 20 to August 7, 2026 to certify additional moving companies into the Defense Personal Property Program (PPA open season notice, 2026). That helps next season more than this one.

Practical booking guidance that has not changed with the new agency:

  • Book the day orders drop. Counseling first, then dates. Peak season runs mid-May through August, with June and July the worst weeks to need a truck.
  • Offer flexible pickup dates. Mid-week and mid-month windows clear faster than the end-of-month scramble everyone else asks for.
  • Work the full timeline. My PCS checklist sequences the moving pieces against the housing pieces month by month, and the complete PCS-to-Pensacola guide covers the destination end.

From the Pensacola end, I will add one local observation: this area's inbound flow never really stops. NAS Pensacola cycles flight students and A-school sailors year-round, so local delivery crews stay busy even in the shoulder months. Every summer I watch the same pattern: the families who booked pack-outs in March and April got their windows, and the families who started in June took whatever was left. If your report date is next summer, your moving timeline starts this winter.

How Much Weight Can You Ship on a 2026 PCS?

Your household goods weight allowance is set by pay grade and dependency status under Joint Travel Regulations Table 5-37, and it did not change with the PPA stand-up. Allowances run from 5,000 pounds for junior enlisted without dependents up to 18,000 pounds for O-6 and above. An E-5 with dependents rates 9,000 pounds; an O-3 with dependents rates 14,500 pounds.

Here is the part the table does not tell you, learned across my own 11 PCS moves: a fully furnished four-bedroom house with a garage blows past 11,000 pounds faster than you think. The E-6 with dependents allowance sounds generous until the crew weighs the truck.

Go over and you pay the government's rate for every excess pound, billed after the fact. Purge hard before pack-out, get an honest weight estimate at counseling, and remember that the stuff you shed in Pensacola's summer heat is stuff you do not pay to store or claim later.

Is a PPM (DITY Move) Still Worth It in 2026?

Sometimes, but run the math at the current rate before you commit. A personally procured move now pays 100 percent of the government constructed cost (GCC), which is what the government would have paid a commercial mover for your weight and distance. The temporary 130 percent incentive that made 2025 PPMs unusually lucrative expired September 30, 2025 (Defense Travel Management Office), so the payout stories you heard last summer are 30 points out of date.

The mechanics: get the PPM authorized at counseling before you move anything, keep certified empty and full weight tickets, save every receipt, and file your settlement afterward. Your documented moving expenses (truck rental, boxes, fuel, tolls) are reimbursed without tax withholding.

Whatever remains above your expenses is profit, taxed as supplemental income with 22 percent withheld at payment (Military OneSource PPM fact sheet, March 2026). Some of those costs interact with your tax return too; my military PCS tax guide walks through what active-duty members can still deduct on a PCS.

The downsides and the partial PPM play

The honest downsides, because a PPM is not free money. You carry all the risk: there is no claims process when you break your own television. Truck rental rates along the Gulf Coast peak in exactly the June-July window when most people move.

A partial PPM is often the smarter play for Pensacola-bound families: let the government move the bulk, and haul the high-value, hard-to-claim items (tools, firearms paperwork done properly, the things you need the first week) yourself. You are paid on the weight you move either way.

What Happens if Your Household Goods Are Lost or Damaged?

Note visible loss or damage on the paperwork at delivery, then submit your formal notice through the move system within 180 days of delivery. After that, file the itemized claim with your moving company. Shipments picked up on or after May 15, 2026 get 12 months from delivery to file for full replacement value, up from nine months under the old rule (Military OneSource, 2026).

That extended window is one of the concrete wins of the PPA era, and it exists because so many families ran out the old clock while fighting HomeSafe-era claims. Protect yourself before the truck ever arrives:

  • Photograph everything. A room-by-room video walkthrough plus close-ups and serial numbers on electronics, tools, and furniture, taken before pack-out day.
  • Declare high-value items in writing. The high-value inventory is your leverage later; do not let a rushed crew skip it.
  • Open the expensive boxes first. Check high-value items during or immediately after delivery while the damage story is fresh and documented.
  • Watch the clock. Miss the full replacement value window and you can still transfer the claim to your military claims office within two years, but you will be paid depreciated value.

How Should Moving Uncertainty Shape Your Pensacola Housing Timeline?

Build slack on both ends. Even in the rebuilt system, a household goods delivery can slip a week or two, and the worst version of that slip is one where your closing date, lease start, and lodging plan all assumed perfection. This is where the moving system and the housing decision actually connect, and where I spend most of my time with PCS clients.

Know your cushion entitlements first, because they define how much slip you can absorb:

  • TLE now covers up to 21 days for a CONUS move, split however you need between the losing and gaining station, a change from 14 days effective November 27, 2024 (Defense Travel Management Office). It reimburses lodging plus meals up to a daily cap (roughly $290 per day under the JTR). Do not burn 15 of those days at your old duty station; the Pensacola end is where the delivery risk lives.
  • Dislocation Allowance rose 3.8 percent for 2026, paying roughly $1,019 to $6,386 depending on grade and dependency status (2026 DLA tables, DFAS). It helps, but it will not cover a month of double housing.
  • Know your on-base and off-base lodging options before you need them. My Pensacola military lodging guide covers the TLE-friendly options near NAS Pensacola, Corry Station, and NASP Saufley, including which fill up in flight-school surge months.

Structuring the deal around the truck

Then build the housing plan around the move, not the other way around. When I put a buyer under contract, I look at the pickup window and the required delivery date before we pick a closing date, and I structure the deal so the keys arrive a few days before the truck can.

Tools I use constantly in this market:

  • flexible closing dates negotiated up front
  • seller leasebacks when we need the house but the sellers need time
  • rate locks with enough runway to survive a two-week slip (covered in my VA loan field manual)
  • a backup lodging plan written into the timeline rather than improvised at 2100 on delivery day

If you are shopping from your current duty station, my sight-unseen buying process exists precisely because most of my clients cannot house-hunt in person, and you can start scanning inventory now on the live MLS search by base with your 2026 BAH rate as the budget anchor.

One more Pensacola-specific note: if you are PCSing out of here rather than in, the same uncertainty argues for deciding early whether to rent out or sell your current home. A sale that closes after you have already reported to the next duty station is manageable; I handle several every season. A vacant house you meant to list in June that is still sitting in August is a carrying cost you did not budget for.

The Bottom Line: What Should You Do Right Now?

The Personal Property Activity is the government's attempt to fix a broken experiment, and the early signs are reasonable: clearer accountability, a familiar booking system, a longer claims window, and pickup volume running slightly ahead of last year. It is not magic, and peak-season capacity is still finite. For your 2026 or 2027 move to Pensacola, the playbook is:

  1. Contact your transportation office the day orders drop, and take the earliest counseling slot offered.
  2. Build the photo and video inventory before pack-out, and declare high-value items in writing.
  3. If you are considering a PPM, run the math at 100 percent of GCC, not the expired 130 percent rate.
  4. Map your TLE days, DLA, and lodging plan before you commit to any closing or lease date.
  5. Pick a housing timeline with slack in it, and a Realtor who plans for the move that actually happens, not the one on paper.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Personal Property Activity (PPA)?

It is headquartered at Scott Air Force Base, Illinois, reports directly to the Secretary of Defense, and is commanded by Army Maj. Gen. Lance Curtis. It runs the Defense Personal Property Program that schedules and manages every PCS shipment.

What happened to HomeSafe Alliance and the GHC contract?

The Pentagon terminated the Global Household Goods Contract with HomeSafe Alliance for cause on June 18, 2025, after widespread missed pickups, late deliveries, and software problems. Moves reverted to the traditional network of approved moving companies managed through installation transportation offices, now under PPA oversight.

How do I schedule my PCS household goods move in 2026?

Contact your installation transportation office (personal property office) as soon as you have orders, even a soft copy. You will complete counseling, then book through the DoD move system. The PPA's official site at ppa.mil consolidates the guidance, and the 833-MIL-MOVE help line runs 24/7 through September 15 during peak season. Start with a PCS checklist so nothing slips.

The temporary 130 percent rate expired September 30, 2025 (DTMO). Documented expenses are reimbursed untaxed; the remaining profit is taxed as income with 22 percent withheld.

How long do I have to file a claim for lost or damaged household goods?

Submit your notice of loss or damage within 180 days of delivery. For shipments picked up on or after May 15, 2026, you then have 12 months from delivery to file the itemized claim for full replacement value, up from nine months previously (Military OneSource, 2026). How many days of TLE do I get for a CONUS PCS in 2026?

Temporary Lodging Expense covers up to 21 days for a CONUS-to-CONUS move, split however you need between the old and new duty station. That increased from 14 days effective November 27, 2024 (Defense Travel Management Office). Installations designated as housing-shortage locations can authorize up to 60 days.

Should I close on a house before my household goods arrive in Pensacola?

Usually yes, but with slack built in. I aim to get buyers keys a few days before the delivery window opens so the truck has somewhere to unload, while negotiating closing dates and rate locks that survive a one-to-two-week slip.
