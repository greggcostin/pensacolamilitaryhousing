Start with the allowance that actually applies

The published 2026 E-5 with-dependent monthly BAH is $1,863 in FL064, Pensacola, and $2,433 in FL056, Eglin AFB. The without-dependent amounts are $1,644 and $2,157. Verify your actual duty-station ZIP and eligibility with finance rather than choosing the allowance from a home-search address.

Use three separate comparisons

Your income scenario: use actual LES income and other documented income, then account for debts. The monthly income comparison lets you change its illustrative debt ratio and allowance factor. Neither setting is a universal underwriting promise.

Your household scenario: compare the same budget with additional income and with that income temporarily unavailable. For a dual-military household, confirm the correct combination of with- and without-dependent rates; do not assume both members receive the with-dependent amount.

Your BAH comparison: compare the actual monthly allowance with the complete ownership expense. This shows how much must come from other income or how much room remains within that allowance. It does not force BAH to be your maximum housing budget.

Replace purchase-price shortcuts with property costs

A rule such as multiplying BAH by a fixed number cannot account for a different interest rate, tax district, insurance quote or condominium assessment. The ownership calculator computes principal and interest from the loan assumptions, then adds entered tax, insurance and monthly expenses. It also includes maintenance and utilities in the broader household total.

Its starting interest rate is 6.71%, the Freddie Mac conventional 30-year national average for September 3, 2026. That is a dated reference, not a VA rate quote. The default tax, insurance and other expense amounts are invented examples. Replace them before using the result for a real property.

A VA funding fee can increase the financed balance unless an exemption applies or the fee is paid at closing. Verify the correct first-use or subsequent-use band and down payment. Keep initial cash, prepaid expenses, repairs and reserves outside the monthly-payment total so you can see both the up-front and ongoing requirements.

Ask for a buyer-based tax estimate

The seller's tax bill is useful history, but ownership changes and different exemption eligibility can change the future bill. Ask the county property appraiser for the appropriate taxable-value estimate and applicable exemptions. Include non-ad valorem charges. In Florida, school and non-school levies can use different taxable values; do not simply subtract an exemption from the sale price and call the result a final tax bill.

For Gulf Shores, Orange Beach or Foley, use Alabama parcel information. For Pensacola, Navarre, Fort Walton Beach or Destin, use the relevant Florida county and tax district. The two-state ownership worksheet is designed for property-specific comparisons.

Compare your holding period and the next PCS

A comfortable payment does not settle whether buying fits a short assignment. Compare purchase cash, recurring expenses, maintenance and the cost of a future sale. If renting the home after PCS is part of the plan, obtain a property-specific rental assessment and account for vacancy, repairs and management before relying on that option.

Use the buyer timeline to organize lender review and due diligence. If you are selling another home, use the seller proceeds worksheet to keep expected closing proceeds and next-purchase cash separate.
