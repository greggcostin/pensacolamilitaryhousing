Frequently asked questions

How long will my home take to sell in Pensacola?

Correctly priced homes in Escambia and Santa Rosa County typically go under contract in 2 to 6 weeks, then 30 to 45 more days to close with a financed buyer. Overpriced homes sit, and the longer a listing sits, the weaker the offers get. Pricing right from day one is the whole game.

Should I renovate before listing?

Usually less than you think. Deep cleaning, paint, lighting, and curb appeal return their cost; major remodels usually do not. Before you spend a dollar, walk the house with us. We will tell you exactly which projects buyers in your price band will actually pay for.

What is the best time of year to list in Pensacola?

Spring and early summer bring the most buyers, including relocating families timed to school calendars and military orders. But well-priced homes sell year-round here, and lower winter inventory can mean less competition for your listing.

Can I sell if I still have a mortgage?

Yes. Your loan is paid off from the sale proceeds at closing, and you keep the difference. We estimate your net proceeds, after payoff, taxes, and costs, before you list so there are no surprises at the closing table.
