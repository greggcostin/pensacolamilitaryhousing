What does a military relocation realtor do differently?

A civilian agent starts with your price range. A military relocation realtor starts with your orders: report date, BAH, gate you will actually badge through, and how many of the next 36 months you will spend deployed or TDY. The plan comes out different, and so does the house.

  • BAH-first budgeting. Your payment gets built from the 2026 FL064 or FL023 rate down, with taxes, insurance, and the funding fee inside the number, not discovered at closing.
  • Sight-unseen capability. Live video tours, gate-to-driveway commute tests at 0630, inspection attendance, and remote closing. The full playbook is in the sight-unseen guide.
  • VA loan fluency. Zero-down structuring, entitlement math, disability fee exemptions, and assumable VA loans, the 2026 rate hack most agents never check for.
  • Base-gate intelligence. Which NAS Pensacola gate serves which neighborhood, what the Blue Angels practice schedule does to Highway 98, and where flight-school students actually sleep.
  • Insurance and flood reality. Pensacola pricing lives and dies on wind and flood zones. Every recommendation comes with the insurance math attached.

How does the process work on a PCS timeline?

Back-planned from your report date, the same way you plan a movement. Most PCS buyers go from first call to keys in 45 to 60 days, and the search can start months before your household goods do.

  1. 15-minute strategy call. Orders, timeline, BAH, must-haves. Book it here or just text your rank and report date to (850) 266-5005.
  2. Numbers before houses. BAH-to-payment math for your grade, a VA-fluent local lender intro, and pre-approval so your offer carries weight.
  3. Curated search. A hand-built MLS search tuned to your gate, school needs, and payment ceiling. No portal spam.
  4. Tours and offer. In person or live video, then an offer strategy built on days-on-market, insurance quotes, and what the seller's agent tells us without meaning to.
  5. Close and keys. Inspection proxy, remote signing if you are still at your losing station, and closing sequenced against your TLE window.
