Student housing at NAS Pensacola is not a preference question. Your orders say PCS or TDY, your pay grade and years of service put you inside or outside the mandatory unaccompanied housing bracket, and your dependency status decides which allowance applies, if any. This guide lays those rules side by side for Navy A and C school students at Corry Station, Air Force CSO students at the 479th Flying Training Group, and student aviators at Training Air Wings FIVE and SIX.

It then shows what an off-base budget looks like once you actually hold a Certificate of Non-Availability, and what can pull that budget apart. Every figure is the published 2026 FL064 rate, not your entitlement; your leave and earnings statement settles that.

Will I live in the dorms or draw BAH as a student at NAS Pensacola or Corry Station?

Navy Housing's NAS Pensacola page states that most personnel in E-1 through E-3, and E-4s with fewer than four years of service, are required to live in unaccompanied housing, and that E-4s may be required to live on base, space permitting. For an unaccompanied first-term student at Corry Station, the default is a dorm room and BAH-Partial. Your orders, your dependency status and the installation's occupancy decide your case, and only the UH office and your finance office can confirm it.

Under the DoD Financial Management Regulation, a member on active duty is authorized a housing allowance based on grade, location and whether the member has dependents, and a housing allowance generally is not authorized for a member who is assigned to appropriate and adequate Government quarters. That sentence from DoD FMR Volume 7A, Chapter 26 sits behind every dorm assignment, and it is why a course title never establishes an allowance.

The Navy rule is published on the Navy Housing NAS Pensacola page and repeated word for word for NAS Whiting Field. The Corry Station on-base versus off-base guide covers families and permanent party; this article covers students. Air Force and other-service students follow their own rule, covered below.

Is a Pensacola course a PCS or a TDY, and why does that change my housing?

Under the Joint Travel Regulations, a course scheduled for 139 or fewer days (less than 20 weeks) is attended in a TDY status, and a course scheduled for 140 or more days (20 weeks) is a PCS with the course location as the permanent duty station. The status decides which quarters rule and which allowance apply. Read the order type on your actual orders, because the scheduled course length sets the status and the Secretary concerned can designate exceptions.

A member on TDY with no PCS involved keeps the housing allowance for the permanent duty station if it was authorized before the TDY, but a member below E-7 who is assigned quarters at the permanent duty station gets no allowance during the TDY even after vacating those quarters. So a C school student on TDY orders from another command does not pick up the Pensacola rate by arriving here.

A member without dependents in the accession pipeline is not authorized BAH, other than BAH-Partial, while at a training location because Government quarters are assigned there, and for BAH authorization a training location of 20 or more weeks is treated as a permanent duty station. That is the A school student who reports to Corry Station straight from boot camp: expect a dorm assignment, whatever the FL064 table says.

Both rules come from the Joint Travel Regulations, chapter 3 and the FMR housing allowance chapter. Before you read anything else, read three lines on your orders:

  • Order type. PCS or TDY, and the scheduled course length that produced it.
  • Dependents. Whether a dependent is listed on the orders and in DEERS.
  • Reporting command. IWTC Corry Station, the 479th Student Squadron, TW-5 or TW-6, because each has its own check-in and housing instruction.

Which grades must live in unaccompanied housing, Navy versus Air Force?

For the Navy the bracket is E-1 through E-3 and E-4 under four years of service; for the Air Force it is E-1 through E-3 and E-4 under three years; and for Air Force CSO students at the 479th it is every unaccompanied student regardless of rank. The table lays out each status with the rule that governs it and what opens the off-base door.

The Navy student dormitory standard is a shared bedroom with at least 90 net square feet per student and no more than four students per bathroom, and members in a PCS status for a training course of 20 weeks or more are a Priority 2 space-required category alongside first-term E-1 to E-4 Sailors. That standard comes from the CNIC Unaccompanied Housing Operations Manual.

AFI 32-6000 directs installations to house unaccompanied Airmen in grades E-1 through E-3 and E-4 with less than 3 years of service in government-controlled unaccompanied housing, treats pipeline students who are not authorized per diem or an allowance as Priority 1 residents, and allows Priority 1 and 2 personnel to relocate off base with allowances only when utilization exceeds 95 percent. The 2026 guidance memorandum to AFI 32-6000 was not fully readable, so a base supplement may adjust these paragraphs.

What is a Certificate of Non-Availability and when does Corry Station issue one?

Under the CNIC unaccompanied housing manual, an eligible single Sailor who wants to move off base submits a written request to the installation commanding officer through the chain of command, and a Certificate of Non-Availability is issued only when Government quarters are not available, which the manual defines as barracks occupancy of 95 percent or greater. A CNA can be temporary or permanent, and a temporary one can be withdrawn when space opens.

The commander responsible for the quarters has the authority to assign and terminate Government quarters and decides when quarters are adequate for assignment, which is why the same regulation produces different answers at different installations. Corry Station does not publish a student occupancy figure, so nobody outside the UH office can tell you whether the 95 percent line has been crossed.

Navy Housing describes NAS Pensacola unaccompanied housing as roughly 4,530 personnel in 14 buildings, and Corry Station as a training command about 3 miles from NAS Pensacola housing roughly 1,770 personnel in 17 buildings. Capacity is not occupancy; the trigger is measured by the UH office.

  • Who asks: the student, in writing, through the chain of command to the installation CO.
  • What decides it: installation occupancy at check-in, tracked in the Navy's eMH housing system.
  • What you get: a temporary or permanent CNA, or a dorm assignment and BAH-Partial.

For a course attended in TDY status, the schoolhouse commander determines whether meals and lodging are available, and if Government quarters are not available a statement of non-availability must be obtained. That governs TDY lodging and per diem; the PCS student's off-base path runs through the UH office and a CNA. Short stays are covered in the military lodging guide.

Do Air Force CSO students at the 479th Flying Training Group live in the dorms?

The 479th Flying Training Group welcome guide tells CSO students that if they are on unaccompanied orders they will be assigned to the Unaccompanied Officers Quarters on arrival regardless of rank or time in service, while students on accompanied orders may live on or off base and must contact the Housing office before signing a lease or closing on a house. That is command guidance dated 27 August 2026 for Undergraduate CSO Training only; it can change, and it does not describe Air Force enlisted students or the Navy dorms.

The 479th Flying Training Group at NAS Pensacola is the Air Force's only training pipeline for combat systems officers and graduates as many as 400 CSOs a year through the 479th Student Squadron, the 455th and 451st Flying Training Squadrons and the 479th Operations Support Squadron. The unit page links the current Undergraduate CSO Welcome Guide, the document that governs your UOQ assignment.

An accompanied CSO student who elects to live off base after finance in-processing is in the same position as any officer renter. The flight school housing guide covers officer rentals, roommate houses and the purchase question; the NAS Pensacola base page has the full FL064 grade table.

What happens to my allowance if I have dependents but arrive alone?

A member with a dependent is authorized the with-dependents housing allowance when adequate Government quarters are not furnished for the member and the dependent without a rental charge, so a married student housed in a dormitory alone does not lose the with-dependents rate. The dependent must be on your orders and in DEERS, and a new accession with a dependent is paid for the dependent's location, which may not be FL064.

The gap between the E-4 with-dependents rate ($1,794) and the without-dependents rate ($1,521) at FL064 is $273 a month in 2026. That spread is the published difference, not a statement about which rate you are authorized; the finance office and your LES settle that after in-processing.

Temporary Lodging Expense on a PCS to a permanent duty station in the CONUS is authorized for up to 21 days, and a student on a course under 20 weeks in TDY status is not authorized dependent travel allowances. TLE applies only on a PCS; a TDY student draws per diem instead, and the PCS checklist walks through the entitlement paperwork.

What is the 2026 FL064 BAH for a student at NAS Pensacola, Corry Station or Whiting Field?

The published 2026 FL064 rates are $1,521 for an E-4 without dependents and $1,794 with dependents, and every grade's figure is on the BAH rates page. The full table comes from the DTMO 2026 BAH rate archive, re-verified against the official file for this article on September 15, 2026.

  • E-4 without dependents: $1,521 a month
  • E-4 with dependents: $1,794 a month
  • E-5 without dependents: $1,644 a month
  • O-1 without dependents: $1,719 a month

An E-5 without dependents draws $1,644 a month at FL064 in 2026. An O-1 without dependents draws $1,719 a month at FL064 in 2026. An E-5 fleet returnee sits outside the mandatory UH bracket but can still be assigned quarters space-available under Navy priorities, and an unaccompanied CSO student assigned to the UOQ does not draw the O-1 figure at all.

Individual rate protection means a member who keeps the same status receives any published BAH increase but no decrease, and the protection ends on a PCS, a reduction in paygrade or a change in dependency status. A student who is authorized BAH on PCS orders and stays past a January rate change is covered by that protection, as DTMO explains; a move from a dorm to an allowance mid-year is a status change the finance office decides.

A member without dependents who is assigned single-type Government quarters and is not authorized BAH receives BAH-Partial, a small fixed amount tied to the 1997 partial quarters rate for the pay grade rather than to the Pensacola rental market. No official 2026 BAH-Partial table was found for this review, so no amount is printed here; your LES shows it if you receive it.

A BAH figure is a housing allowance and nothing more; it is not a loan approval or a home-price limit. Use the renting on BAH guide to test a rental against your actual LES line, and the Corry Station base page for the rest of the FL064 table.

Once I hold a CNA, should I rent, share or buy?

Rent or share first; buying needs a PCS-length course, a lender and a CNA that is not temporary. The worked example below is a labeled hypothetical, not a market quote, and the downside list explains what can reverse it.

Worked example. In this hypothetical, an E-4 student without dependents who holds a Certificate of Non-Availability and rents a $700 room in a shared house keeps a margin of $821 a month from the $1,521 FL064 allowance before utilities, renter's insurance and commuting costs. The arithmetic is 1,521 minus 700, which equals 821; the $700 rent is invented for illustration and is not a Pensacola market figure.

Before that margin means anything, subtract utilities, renter's insurance, a deposit, fuel for the drive to the gate and any furniture the dorm would have supplied. Then look at the three ways it goes wrong:

  • The CNA can be temporary or refused. A temporary CNA is withdrawn when space opens, and a request is refused outright below 95 percent occupancy, so a lease signed before the certificate is issued is a lease you may have to carry from the dorm.
  • A mid-course move back into the dorm. The commander who controls the quarters can reassign them, and the lease keeps running while you pay for a room you no longer live in.
  • SCRA may not let you out. Finishing a course without new PCS orders is not a statutory trigger, so the lease term, not the course length, decides when you are free.

Under 50 U.S.C. 3955, a servicemember who signs a lease and then receives orders for a permanent change of station, or deployment orders of 90 days or more, may terminate it by delivering written notice and a copy of the orders to the landlord; for a lease with monthly rent the termination takes effect 30 days after the next rent due date following delivery, with rent prorated and no early termination charge. The full text is on Cornell LII.

Corry Station is a separate campus that Navy Housing describes as about 3 miles from NAS Pensacola, with its own unaccompanied housing office at 640 Roberts Ave. If your orders read IWTC Corry Station and you hold a CNA, drive from the candidate rental to Corry's gate at your class report time, not to the NAS Pensacola main gate, because a route that suits one gate does not always suit the other.

The NAS Pensacola gates guide shows which gate serves which command. If you are a PCS student with a CNA that is not temporary, a lender and a course long enough to carry a closing, the purchase question is real, but the BAH figure alone does not answer it. Read on base versus off base at NAS Pensacola first, then bring the numbers to the team.

Where do I check in for housing at NAS Pensacola, Corry Station and Whiting Field?

The NAS Pensacola Navy Housing Service Center is at 1581 Duncan Road, Building 735, Pensacola, FL 32508 (850-452-5168, Pensacola_Housing@us.navy.mil), the Corry Station unaccompanied housing office (Mariner's Roost) is at 640 Roberts Ave, Pensacola, FL 32511 (front desk 850-452-6609), and Navy Housing encourages every unaccompanied member eligible to live off base to check in at the Housing Service Center first.

IWTC Corry Station tells arriving students to report with original orders to the main quarterdeck in Building 3714, where they receive their housing and school instructions; A school students from boot camp are assigned a mentor, fleet returnees and students from other A schools are directed to the Bachelor Enlisted Quarters in Building 1084, and officer students are advised to reserve Navy Gateway Inns and Suites. The IWTC Corry Station reporting page directs you to quarters; it does not say who draws an allowance.

Training Air Wing FIVE sits aboard NAS Whiting Field about 5 miles north of Milton and trains aviators from the Navy, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Air Force and allied nations, while Training Air Wing SIX is headquartered aboard NAS Pensacola and produces Naval Flight Officers, with roughly 300 Navy, Marine Corps and foreign students graduating a year. Neither the TW-5 page nor the TW-6 page publishes housing guidance.

NAS Whiting Field's Housing Service Center is at 570 Merrill Drive, Milton, FL 32570 (850-623-9726), its unaccompanied housing office is at 7426 USS Lexington Circle, Building 2957 (850-623-7095), and Navy Housing describes Whiting Field unaccompanied housing as roughly 40 personnel in one building. Details are on the Navy Housing NAS Whiting Field page and in the Whiting Field off-base housing guide.

Training Air Wing FIVE students report to NAS Whiting Field, which sits in the same FL064 military housing area as NAS Pensacola, so the 2026 rate does not change, but the housing office, the much smaller dorm inventory and the commute do. A Pensacola-side lease that works for a Corry classmate can add a long daily drive to Milton for a Whiting student, so test that route at your actual report time. The Whiting Field base page carries the same FL064 table.

Before you sign anything, work through this list in order.

  1. Read the orders: PCS or TDY, course length, dependents listed, and the reporting command.
  2. Report to the quarterdeck or STUCON first; housing instructions come from the command, not from a listing.
  3. Ask the UH office in writing whether you are space-required and what the current occupancy is; a CNA is issued only at 95 percent or greater.
  4. Check the BAH line on your LES after in-processing with finance; a published FL064 rate is not your entitlement.
  5. Check in at the Housing Service Center (1581 Duncan Road, Building 735 for NAS Pensacola and Corry; 570 Merrill Drive, Milton for Whiting) for lease review and referrals once you are accompanied or hold a CNA.
  6. Test the drive to the actual gate at class report time: Corry's gate for Corry, Whiting from Milton.
  7. Put a military clause in the lease that is broader than the SCRA, and keep the term short.
  8. Budget the room-share margin honestly: $1,521 minus rent, then utilities, insurance, deposit and fuel; a CNA can be temporary.
  9. Consider a purchase only after all of the above, and only with a PCS-length stay and a lender; BAH is not a price limit.

Bring your orders, your dependency status and the housing instruction you received at check-in to the contact page, and the first question will be which row of the table you sit in.
