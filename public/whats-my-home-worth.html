An online estimate can't tell you any of that. It also can't see the things that actually move price on the Gulf Coast: which side of the street the flood zone splits on, what the insurance quote will do to a buyer's payment, whether the comp that closed high had backup offers, or whether your assumable 3% VA note is worth real money to the right buyer. Mine does, because I check every one of those before I give you a number.

PCS Math: Your List Date Works Backward From Your Report Date

In the current Pensacola-area market, a well-priced home takes roughly 45-75 days from list to close: figure two to four weeks to a solid contract, then a 30-45 day escrow on a financed buyer. Add prep time (photos, punch-list, staging) and the safe planning window is 90 days before you need to be gone. If your report date is closer than that, you still have options (leaseback after closing, selling occupied, a cash-offer floor, or renting it out), and my valuation lays out each path with real numbers, not theory.

Deciding between renting it out and selling? That decision hinges on your equity, your interest rate, and honest rental math. I wrote the full framework here: Rent or Sell When You PCS. If you want the floor established first, see how cash offers really compare to listing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast will I get my valuation?

Two speeds. Instant: the RealScout home-value tracker on this page gives you a live MLS-based estimate immediately and re-checks it monthly. Realtor-grade: my hand-built CMA lands in your inbox within 24 hours on business days, usually the same day, built personally from Pensacola MLS data, nothing outsourced.

What's the difference between the RealScout estimate and your CMA?

RealScout tracks your home against live MLS activity in your neighborhood. Great for watching your equity over time. My CMA is the decision-grade number: I adjust for condition, flood-zone splits, insurance impact, and your PCS timeline, and I include the net-proceeds and rent-vs-sell math. Use RealScout to watch; use the CMA to act.

Is it really free, with no obligation?

Yes. No listing agreement, no commitment, no follow-up pressure. Most PCS families ask for the number 3-6 months before orders drop so they can plan. That's exactly the right way to use it.

Zillow already gives me a number. Why would this be different?

Automated models can't see condition, flood-zone splits on the same street, insurance-driven price cuts, or what backup offers looked like on the comp that closed. In the Pensacola market those factors routinely move value by $15,000-40,000, more than most AVM error bands.

Should I sell or rent it out when I PCS?

It depends on your equity, your current interest rate, and whether your loan is assumable. My valuation includes the rent-vs-sell math both ways (projected rent, management cost, vacancy, and what selling nets you today) so you decide with numbers instead of guesses.

I already PCS'd / I'm OCONUS. Can you still help?

Yes. I've sold homes for owners in Japan, Germany, and deployed locations. Photos, prep, contractor access, closing by power of attorney or remote online notarization. I've made 11 PCS moves myself and built my listing process for exactly this.

My VA loan has a low rate. Does that change my home's value?

It can. A 2.75-3.5% assumable VA loan is a marketable asset in 2026: marketed correctly to qualified buyers, it can justify a premium or a faster sale. I check assumability on every valuation. See my assumable VA loans guide for how that works.
