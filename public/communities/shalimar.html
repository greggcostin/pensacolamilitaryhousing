Shalimar, FL: The Quiet Bayou Town Two Miles from Eglin's West Gate

A 0.29-square-mile town on Choctawhatchee Bay's bayous, platted for Eglin officers in the 1940s, and still one of the shortest Eglin commutes on the Emerald Coast.

Gregg Costin
Retired USAF Combat Systems Officer · Realtor at Levin Rinke Realty (FL & AL) · MRP · ABR · RENE
Reviewed & updated · July 2026

Shalimar is a tiny Okaloosa County town fronting Choctawhatchee Bay's bayous about two miles from Eglin AFB's West Gate. It offers one of the shortest military commutes anywhere on the Emerald Coast, roughly 70% owner-occupied housing, and Fort Walton Beach (FL023) BAH rates, with the 2026-27 Okaloosa school rezoning now in effect. If you are PCSing to Eglin and want quiet streets over big-box convenience, this pocket between Fort Walton Beach and the base deserves a hard look.

Key Facts (as of July 2026)

  • Shalimar covers just 0.29 square miles; population was 737 at the 2020 census, with an ACS 2024 five-year estimate of 885.
  • Eglin AFB's 24/7 West Gate is about 2 miles away: roughly 4-10 minutes, one of the shortest Eglin commutes of any off-base community.
  • Hurlburt Field is about 11 miles from Shalimar, roughly 24 minutes in light traffic and 25-35 minutes at peak.
  • The ACS 2024 median owner-occupied home value is $476,500; median household income is $115,417.
  • Per Redfin, ZIP 32579 (broader than the town itself) had a median sale price of about $370,000 in March 2026, on a small 38-home sample.
  • Shalimar sits in the Fort Walton Beach BAH area (FL023), which pays higher 2026 rates than Pensacola's FL064.

Where Shalimar Is: 0.29 Square Miles on the Bayous South of Eglin

Shalimar sits immediately south of Eglin AFB, wedged between Fort Walton Beach and Niceville in the Choctawhatchee Bay system: Garnier's Bayou runs along its west side and Poquito Bayou to the north. At 0.29 square miles, it is one of the smallest incorporated towns in Northwest Florida: 737 residents at the 2020 census, with the ACS 2024 five-year estimate at 885 people in roughly 406 households.

Small does not mean sleepy on the government side. Okaloosa County keeps a real footprint here (sheriff's offices and a county administration building are located in Shalimar), so the town punches above its weight in county affairs. But the day-to-day feel is exactly what the map suggests: a quiet residential pocket of sidewalked streets and bayou water views, minutes from the gate, with the shopping and dining of Fort Walton Beach and Eglin Parkway right outside town limits.

Commuting from Shalimar: Two Miles to Eglin's West Gate

This is the headline reason military families pick Shalimar. Eglin has two gates open 24/7: the West Gate off FL-85 (Eglin Parkway), which is the closest to Shalimar and Fort Walton Beach, and the East Gate off FL-397 on the Valparaiso side. The North and ACC gates only open on weekdays, 0600-0800 and 1600-1800.

From Shalimar, the West Gate is about 2 miles out: roughly 4 minutes in free-flow traffic, call it 4-10 in the real world. Very few addresses off base beat that. If your assignment leans toward the Valparaiso side of the reservation, the East Gate run is roughly 6-8 miles, about 12-18 minutes.

A Town Built for Eglin Officers: 1947 History and Today's Character

Shalimar's military DNA is literal. Developer Clifford H. Meigs platted the town in 1943-44 and built 160 homes specifically to house military officers stationed at Eglin. It incorporated as a municipality in June 1947 with Meigs as its first mayor. The middle school in town still carries his name.

Nearly eighty years later, the census data still reads like a field-grade neighborhood roster: 20.5% of residents are veterans, 58.4% hold a bachelor's degree or higher, the median age is 53.8, and the median household income is $115,417 (all ACS 2024 five-year figures).

Day to day, the town's amenities are modest and residential: Shalimar Park offers about eight acres of walking paths and open space, the streets are sidewalked, and the waterfront runs along Garnier's Bayou and Poquito Bayou. The Shalimar Pointe area is built around an 18-hole golf course near Choctawhatchee Bay. There is no commercial strip inside town. That is the point, and Eglin Parkway's shopping is minutes away.

Shalimar Schools: Shalimar Elementary, Meigs Middle, Choctawhatchee High

Shalimar addresses are typically zoned to Shalimar Elementary (PK-5, inside town), Clifford Meigs Middle School at 150 Richbourg Ave in Shalimar, and Choctawhatchee High School at 110 Racetrack Rd NW in Fort Walton Beach. There is a neat historical loop here: Choctawhatchee High originally opened in Shalimar in 1952, and when it moved, its old Shalimar campus became Meigs Middle.

One 2026 development matters for buyers. On February 24, 2026, the Okaloosa County School Board voted unanimously (Resolution 26-03) to close Mary Esther Elementary and Longwood Elementary at the end of the 2025-26 school year, citing a 19.3% decade-long enrollment decline in south-county schools.

Beginning fall 2026, Shalimar Elementary is one of six south-zone schools receiving those rezoned students; the district published new attendance-zone maps and coordinated the change with military leadership at Eglin and Hurlburt. No middle or high school zone changes were reported. Bottom line: verify any specific address with the district's school site locator before you sign. I do this on every Okaloosa transaction.

Homes and Prices in Shalimar (as of Mid-2026)

Shalimar is a premium micro-market with structurally thin inventory: you cannot build more of a 0.29-square-mile bayou town. The ACS 2024 median owner-occupied home value is $476,500, well above neighboring Fort Walton Beach figures.

On the sales side, per Redfin data, ZIP 32579 posted a median sale price of about $370,000 in March 2026, down 20.3% year over year, but that ZIP covers more territory than the town itself, and the figure rests on just 38 sales, so treat it as loose context rather than gospel.

Median price per square foot in the ZIP was $224, up 10.3% over the same period. Housing stock ranges from the original mid-century officer cottages to bayou-front homes and the Shalimar Pointe golf-course community.

Renting for a short tour is possible but competitive: roughly 30% of the town's ~406 households rent (about 70% owner-occupied, per ACS 2024). If you go month-to-month at the end of a lease, Florida law (F.S. 83.57) requires 30 days' notice to terminate, useful flexibility when your follow-on assignment drops late.

Shalimar falls under the Fort Walton Beach military housing area (FL023), which pays higher 2026 BAH rates than Pensacola's FL064. Check your grade on my 2026 BAH rates table. Your rate follows your duty station, Eglin or Hurlburt, not which street you live on.

Shalimar's price point means it typically pencils best for mid-career and senior buyers, or dual-income households. And for anyone planning to stay past one tour, the thin-supply dynamics work in an owner's favor.

How Shalimar Compares to Fort Walton Beach and Niceville

Think of it as a three-way trade around the Eglin gates.

  • Fort Walton Beach gives you far more inventory, more price points, and the area's commercial core (Shalimar's own zoned high school, Choctawhatchee, is there) at the cost of busier streets.
  • Niceville and Bluewater Bay, across the bayou system, are the classic East Gate play with their own strong following.
  • Shalimar is the smallest and quietest of the three: fewer homes for sale at any given moment and a higher median value, in exchange for the shortest West Gate commute of the group and a genuinely small-town feel with county government, not tourist traffic, as the civic anchor.

For price context at the resort end of the county, see Destin. Shalimar buys you bay-area water access without beach-town pricing dynamics or crowds.

FAQ: Living in Shalimar, FL

How long is the commute from Shalimar to Eglin AFB?

Very short. Eglin's 24/7 West Gate, off FL-85/Eglin Parkway, is about 2 miles from Shalimar, roughly 4-10 minutes. The East Gate on the Valparaiso side (FL-397) is roughly 6-8 miles, about 12-18 minutes.

Can I live in Shalimar and work at Hurlburt Field?

Yes, but expect a longer drive than Eglin commuters: about 11 miles and roughly 24 minutes in light traffic, 25-35 minutes at peak, crossing Fort Walton Beach via US-98 or Racetrack Road.

What schools serve Shalimar?

Shalimar addresses are typically zoned to Shalimar Elementary (in town), Clifford Meigs Middle School (also in Shalimar, on the original 1952 Choctawhatchee High campus), and Choctawhatchee High School in Fort Walton Beach. Shalimar Elementary is receiving some rezoned students in 2026-27, so verify your specific address with the Okaloosa County School District locator.

What do homes cost in Shalimar?

Shalimar is small and trades at a premium for the area: the ACS 2024 median owner-occupied home value is $476,500, and per Redfin, ZIP 32579 sales ran about $370,000 in March 2026 on a small sample of 38 homes (the ZIP covers more than the town itself). Ranges shift, so treat these as broad context.

Which BAH rate applies in Shalimar?

Shalimar is in the Fort Walton Beach military housing area (FL023), which pays higher 2026 BAH rates than Pensacola's FL064: an E-5 with dependents draws about $2,433 per month and an O-3 about $3,399. Your rate follows your duty station (Eglin AFB or Hurlburt Field), not your home address within the area.

Are there rentals in Shalimar for a short PCS tour?

Some, but inventory is thin. About 30% of Shalimar's roughly 406 households rent (about 70% owner-occupied per ACS 2024), and the town covers only 0.29 square miles, so rentals move fast. Florida month-to-month tenancies require 30 days' notice under F.S. 83.57.

Which Eglin AFB gates are open 24/7?

Two: the West Gate off FL-85 (Eglin Parkway), closest to Shalimar and Fort Walton Beach, and the East Gate off FL-397 on the Valparaiso side. The North and ACC gates open only on weekdays, 0600-0800 and 1600-1800.
