YOUR PART OF THE COAST

Start with how you will use the coast.

Harbor activity, a Gulf outlook and a convenient daily routine are different priorities. Distinguish the City of Destin from nearby areas marketed under the broader destination name. Establish the actual parcel, county, association and allowed use, then compare homes with the same ownership form and condition rather than treating a regional average as a price for your shortlist.

THE PLACES BETWEEN HOME AND EVERYDAY LIFE

Put the area into context.

Destin Harbor Boardwalk

The city describes its boardwalk as nearly a quarter mile of waterfront access connecting harbor businesses and activity. Check current parking and access for the part of the harbor you intend to use.

Henderson Beach State Park

The park introduced required day-use reservations on May 15, 2026. Its official page also carries current access and maintenance notices, so check it before planning a visit.

Plan the actual trip.

Test US-98 and the actual approach to your home at the time you would use them. Compare Fort Walton Beach, Niceville and the specific work destination rather than assigning one drive time to all of Destin. An attractive beach view does not establish the weekday trip.

CLEAR ANSWERS BEFORE THE NEXT STEP

Destin questions, answered.

Does a Destin condo automatically allow short-term rentals?

No. Confirm the specific property jurisdiction, applicable registration requirements and association restrictions before relying on a proposed rental use.

Do I need a reservation for Henderson Beach State Park?

The park introduced required day-use reservations on May 15, 2026. Check the official park page for current availability and access instructions.

How should I compare two coastal condos?

Compare units in the context of their buildings: financing, insurance, dues, reserves, assessments, inspection information, access and use restrictions all affect the decision.

Can a VA buyer purchase a Destin condo?

A VA-backed purchase can finance an eligible condo in a VA-approved project for qualifying personal occupancy. Approval of the condominium project, the borrower and the specific transaction are separate checks. Gregg Costin helps buyers examine the unit and association documents while their lender confirms financing.

Request the budget, reserves, assessments, insurance and use restrictions for the particular association. Compare those costs with the unit price and your actual Eglin or Hurlburt route before deciding whether a Destin condo fits your move.
