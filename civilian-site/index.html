Frequently asked questions

How do I get started buying or selling with The Costin Team?

Call or text (850) 266-5005, or send a message through the contact page. The first step is a 15-minute conversation about your goals, timeline, and budget. No pressure and no obligation.

What areas does The Costin Team cover?

All of Escambia and Santa Rosa County: Pensacola, Gulf Breeze, Pace, Milton, Cantonment, Navarre, and Perdido Key, plus the Fort Walton Beach, Niceville, and Destin corridor. Gregg is licensed in both Florida and Alabama.

What does it cost to work with a buyer's agent?

Buyer-agent compensation is negotiable and agreed in writing up front. In many transactions it can be covered by the seller or built into the offer. We walk through exactly how it works before you tour a single home.

How is my home's list price determined?

With a comparative market analysis built from recent closed sales, active competition, and current buyer demand in your neighborhood, not an automated estimate. You get the data behind the number and a pricing strategy matched to your timeline.
