Every cost assumption is visible

The model uses zero down, a financed 2.15% first-use VA purchase funding fee, a 30-year term and 6.71% interest. The rate is Freddie Mac's September 3 conventional national average, used as a transparent example; it is not a VA offer or an APR. Fee exemptions and a different down payment change the math.

  • Annual property taxes: $3,600, including assumed tax-bill assessments.
  • Annual homeowners and wind insurance combined: $4,800; annual flood insurance: $1,200.
  • Monthly association dues: $75; other recurring assessments: $0; mortgage insurance: $0 for this VA illustration.
  • Monthly maintenance reserve: $250; utilities: $225.

Monthly total = loan principal and interest + annual tax and premiums divided by 12 + monthly dues, other assessments, maintenance and utilities. The median is the arithmetic mean of the 13th and 14th sorted monthly totals. Costs are calculated before display rounding. Initial cash, closing charges, prepaid escrow, repairs and future cost increases need a separate budget.

Replace the assumptions in the ownership calculator or compare two actual homes and export your worksheet. Use actual LES and household income when comparing the result with your budget.

County, ZIP, city and duty station are different geographies

The table preserves Zillow's city label and separately lists counties intersecting the matching Census 2020 ZCTA. ZCTAs are statistical approximations of ZIP delivery areas, not parcel jurisdictions. Three identifiers cross county lines: 32539 and 32578 intersect Okaloosa and Walton; 32561 intersects Escambia and Santa Rosa. These relationships do not establish city limits, school assignment, flood zone, taxing district or military reporting location.

Do not copy a seller's historical tax bill into a new buyer's budget. Confirm the parcel's county and jurisdiction, buyer-based assessment and exemptions, non-ad valorem charges and any lease fees. Obtain insurance quotes for the address and intended use, and review association budgets and assessments. A flat illustrative figure cannot establish which ZIP has the lowest actual insurance or taxes.

Use the index to ask better property questions

  1. Pick specific homes and record asking price and recent comparable closed sales separately from ZHVI.
  2. Request a lender Loan Estimate and confirmation of your VA fee and entitlement scenario.
  3. Collect buyer-based tax information, insurer quotes and association documents dated for that property.
  4. Compare complete costs with your actual income, savings and debts, then check the actual reporting route and next-PCS options.
