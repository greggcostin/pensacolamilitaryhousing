Verify each allowance before combining the budget

For two BAH-eligible members married with children, the DoD rule is one with-dependent allowance and one without-dependent allowance. For two E-5s using the published Pensacola FL064 rates, that is $1,863 + $1,644 = $3,507 per month. For the same illustration in FL056, it is $2,433 + $2,157 = $4,590.

These examples assume both members are eligible for those rates. Different grades, assignments, government housing, dependency situations or individual rate protection require separate checks. Use each member's finance-confirmed entitlement and LES. Do not double the with-dependent rate simply because two members are buying.

Separate the household budget from the loan structure

Start with the costs you want to carry comfortably. In the income and BAH worksheet, compare documented income with and without the additional income. For two military members, ask the lender how each member's taxable and nontaxable income is counted rather than entering a second BAH payment as taxable salary.

Then ask the lender to compare the available loan structures, entitlement use and any required down payment. A married military couple, a military member with a civilian spouse and unmarried co-buyers do not automatically have the same underwriting treatment. A COE establishes eligibility; it does not approve a particular loan amount or property.

If either member has an existing VA loan, collect its current balance and entitlement information. A one-time restoration while keeping the prior home requires its VA loan to be paid in full. Keeping an active VA loan calls for a remaining-entitlement review. Use the reviewed VA purchase guide and VA's eligibility and restoration guidance for that conversation.

Choose a home that works for both assignments

List the actual work locations, gates and expected schedules. Compare travel in both directions at the relevant times. Two people assigned to NAS Pensacola face a different decision from a household splitting duties between Whiting Field and Hurlburt. A city label or an advertised drive time is not enough to establish the route that works for both people.

Compare the mortgage payment and every property expense before narrowing the search. A coastal condominium with lower principal and interest may carry higher dues, insurance responsibilities or assessments. Use the ownership calculator for the loan payment, then the two-home comparison worksheet to compare complete budgets and initial cash.

Plan the next move while you can make decisions together

Discuss how you would handle a PCS affecting one or both members, an income change or a decision to separate ownership. Ask the closing attorney or title professional to explain the proposed ownership form and obtain legal advice on any agreement between co-buyers. A website cannot choose a title structure for your circumstances.

Model at least three situations: both current incomes continue; only one income remains for a period; and the property is vacant while you sell or prepare to rent. Include cash reserves, insurance for the intended use, management expenses and repairs. Do not count a future tenant's rent as accepted qualifying income without lender review.

Gregg can help coordinate a local plan using the PCS sell-or-rent guide, seller proceeds worksheet and the actual property's documents. Agreeing on an exit process before closing helps you identify the questions that need professional advice while both members have time to address them.

Frequently asked questions

Do two married military members both receive BAH with dependents?

For two BAH-eligible service members married with children, DoD states that one receives the with-dependent rate and the other receives the without-dependent rate. Confirm the allocation and each member's eligibility with finance.

How much BAH would two E-5s receive in the Pensacola example?

Using one 2026 FL064 with-dependent rate of $1,863 and one without-dependent rate of $1,644 gives $3,507 per month. Using the equivalent FL056 rates, $2,433 plus $2,157 gives $4,590. Actual assignments, dependency and eligibility can differ.

Does combined BAH establish a joint VA loan approval?

No. Both applicants' documented income, debts and the proposed property need lender review. Eligibility, entitlement and occupancy are separate from the household's preferred budget. Do not assume that combining benefits doubles a fixed purchase limit.

What happens if we already have an active VA loan?

Ask the lender to review the existing entitlement use and remaining entitlement before selecting the new loan structure. VA's one-time restoration while retaining a home requires the previous VA loan to be paid in full; an active loan is a different situation.

How should a dual-military couple choose an area?

Confirm both work destinations and expected schedules, then compare actual routes and property costs. An assignment at Whiting Field and one at Hurlburt is a different routing problem from two assignments at NAS Pensacola.

What should we plan before the next PCS?

Model keeping both incomes, losing one income and carrying the home during a vacancy. Discuss financing obligations with the lender, ownership and exit arrangements with your closing professional, and a realistic sale or rental plan with Gregg.
