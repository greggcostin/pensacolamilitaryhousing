# Santa Rosa school-guide source register

Checked: September 6, 2026. Scope: all 29 Santa Rosa County records with an existing `/schools/` report URL in the current school-finder dataset. Companion editorial: `content/schools/report-editorial-santarosa.json`.

Research used school/district publications and the existing NCES identity/grade-span records. Each editorial record contains a school-specific overview, two source-linked highlights, enrollment guidance, two questions for a school visit, and source URLs with check dates. Performance comparisons and alternative-school calculations are rendered separately from the official accountability dataset.

The material deliberately does not infer reputation, school quality, instructional effectiveness, selective admission or activity availability from demographics, geography, a mascot, or a single award. Published program listings are opportunities to verify with counselors, not guarantees of places or a specific timetable. Historical events are described as historical examples rather than annual promises.

## Evidence limits and reconciliations

- **Santa Rosa Virtual Franchise:** the NCES 2024–25 directory says K–12, but [the district's July 8, 2026 announcement](https://www.santarosaschools.org/live_feeds/12620247) advertises full-time Santa Rosa Online for grades 3–12. [The school counseling page](https://www.santarosaschools.org/o/sro/page/courses-available) agrees. The editorial distinguishes this from [part-time franchise courses](https://www.santarosaschools.org/live-feed?page_no=4). The directory's Firehouse Road location is administrative and must not be represented as a daily school commute.
- **Chumuckla and Wallace Lake:** current school introductions emphasize K–6 and K–8 respectively; dated federal records additionally include pre-K. The editorial uses the school introduction for the main regular-grade description and does not promise pre-K admission.
- **Jay Elementary:** some official pages returned only navigation. A cached homepage footer incorrectly said Navarre, while the current official contact and federal directory identify Jay. Editorial uses the verified Jay address and [school-published activity updates](https://bes.santarosaschools.org/o/jes/live-feed?page_no=7) rather than guessing a specialty.
- **Jay High:** the CTE page was empty in the retrievable view. Building Construction, Culinary and Agriculture were verified in a [district school spotlight](https://pdc.santarosaschools.org/live-feed/?page_no=28). Current entry/course availability must be checked with guidance.
- **West Navarre Intermediate:** the individual ACES Garden Club and PBIS pages were not retrievable. Activity names are visible in [official school navigation](https://www.santarosaschools.org/o/wni); Community Reveal and model PBIS context come from an [August 2025 district update](https://www.santarosaschools.org/live-feed?page_no=25).
- **Holley-Navarre Intermediate:** its House System detail page was mostly navigation; the active program and 2026 House Cup example are independently described in [school updates](https://www.santarosaschools.org/o/hni/live-feed?page_no=1). No unverified house-system design details are asserted.
- **Martin Luther King Middle:** the linked club PDF could not be opened. Amazing Shake mentoring and the academic-team example are supported by school-published news instead.
- Several Apptegy school pages have equivalent district and school-subdomain URLs. The source list preserves the exact URL that returned supporting content, including cross-school subdomain aliases which still display the correct school organization. Top-level `schoolWebsite` links use the district's `/o/{school}` route.
- School sites sometimes retain older rating slogans or stale enrollment counts. Editorial omits those counts and slogans; the dated Florida accountability data remains the source for academic grades and comparisons.
