Request: add useful installation contacts and distinguish school, service and assignment-specific reporting routes in the nine PCS guides. This is a local draft update to the client collection, not a website deployment or a claim of search improvement.

The structured contact records and exact primary-source URLs are in `../base-directories.mjs`. Sources were opened or located in the September 7 web research session. Phone calls, email delivery and appointment availability were not tested. Public organizational inboxes are included where published; no guessed email formats or private contact records were used. Sources that required an authenticated directory were not bypassed.

## Editorial decisions

- NAS Pensacola has separate NAS permanent-party, NASC/NIFE, NATTC, MATSG and Air Force 479 FTG/479 STUS routes. The 479 FTG CSS number in current Military OneSource reporting information is the primary CSO contact. The June 27, 2022 welcome packet is explicitly archival; its Building 746 listing is qualified and no old reimbursement, fitness or COVID policy was carried forward.
- NASC's public Aviation Training School page retains legacy COVID instructions. The directory gives Flight Management and the quarterdeck, and asks for current onboarding instead of reproducing those rules.
- IWTC Corry's reporting page gives both 6:15 and 7 a.m. next-workday processing times. Neither was silently selected. The guide explains the conflict and directs the reader to the quarterdeck. Marine Detachment Corry has a separately sourced reporting card. Other-service members are told to follow their gaining-service instructions; old third-party 313 TRS listings were not copied.
- NETPDC has conflicting public addresses: its current Contact Us page lists 130 West Avenue, while another official page still lists Saufley Field. The guide does not claim a confirmed relocation or universal Saufley reporting counter. DANTES and MilGears are described as online education resources, not walk-in check-in desks.
- NAS ID services at 421 Saufley Street are explicitly distinguished from Saufley Field. Government housing and privatized housing staff were not conflated.
- Naval Hospital Pensacola's current notice routes access through Corry Station gate 10. Its urgent-care page states it has no emergency room. Both qualifications stay beside the medical contact.
- Whiting's Military OneSource HHG page reports a temporary local office closure and directs customers to Pensacola. That source has a seven-day review interval. Whiting flight students and CNATT technical students have separate cards; no old daily muster schedule was copied.
- Eglin's current MPF page supplies the ID contact instead of an older general-directory number and contains September 2026 reduced-service notices. It also has a seven-day review interval.
- Duke's current DEERS page lists 850-883-6433 and appointment-only service. It takes precedence over an older locator number. Shared Eglin services are clearly identified as a separate trip, with eligibility and unit-routing confirmation.
- A misleading emergency-room entry in the Hurlburt FSS directory was excluded. Medical contact information comes from the official Air Force resource locator. Emergency instructions use 911.
- Tyndall's January 14, 2026 official phone list is combined with current MPF, School Liaison and childcare pages. Conflicting finance and lodging building references are marked for telephone confirmation rather than inventing a resolved address. Current sourced addresses and published email are included for the school and youth contacts.
- NSA Panama City's FFSC program page lists 850-230-7300 while the installation directory lists 850-235-5800. Both are labeled, and the reader is told to confirm the office. Its branch clinic at 2500 Veterans Way is explicitly separate from the installation command address. Navy, Marine and Air Force NDSTC training contacts are separated. NEDU and NSWC assignments are not sent to the dive-school desk by default.

## Maintenance and acceptance

Contact source dates are September 7. The existing financial edition and biography dates are not advanced. Normal directory review is due after 30 days; temporary service arrangements after seven days. `scripts/client-guide-freshness.mjs` records these intervals but never advances a review date automatically. A reported wrong number, changed command, gate change or office move requires immediate source review regardless of the queue date.

The HTML/PDF build keeps citations beside the individual contact, clickable telephone/email links, a correct contents page and the approved personal brand page last. Directory references need not be duplicated in the general bibliography. Existing photo selection and substantive source blocks are preserved. Dedicated directory checks compare against the saved pre-edit collection and independently read PDF text and link annotations. Manual review must cover every current guide contact sheet, new directory pages, a numerical example, BAH table, worksheet and mobile long-link example before packaging.
