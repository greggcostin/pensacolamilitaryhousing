Which BAH area applies to your assignment?

Pensacola uses FL064 for the NAS Pensacola, Corry Station, Saufley Field and NAS Whiting Field examples. FL056, Eglin AFB, covers the Eglin, Hurlburt Field and Duke Field examples. BAH eligibility normally follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, pay grade and dependency status. The address of a home you are considering does not by itself change your allowance.

Use the official DoD lookup and your finance office to confirm your personal entitlement. The published E-5 with-dependent difference between these two areas is $570 per month. That is an allowance difference, not a guaranteed difference in buying power.

How to use the result before you make an offer

Begin with a monthly budget that leaves room for debts, savings and your other household expenses. Compare it with the full cost of the property. Then ask the lender to review documented income, credit, entitlement, residual income and the home itself. A budget calculation and a lending decision answer different questions.

The funding-fee selector follows the VA purchase bands: first use below 5% down is 2.15%; subsequent use below 5% is 3.3%; 5% to less than 10% is 1.5%; and 10% or more is 1.25%. Confirm any exemption with your lender. A financed fee increases the loan balance; a fee paid at closing belongs in your separate initial-cash plan.

Taxes and insurance are address-specific. Enter a buyer-based annual tax estimate that includes applicable charges, plus actual coverage quotes. Do not assume the seller's homestead savings transfer to you. For Alabama, use the Alabama parcel's tax information; this tool does not apply a Florida exemption or an Escambia County tax rate.

For a condominium, use the condominium document checklist to identify dues, assessments, insurance responsibilities and reserve questions. Keep one-time special assessments and initial repairs in your cash plan if they are not recurring monthly charges.
