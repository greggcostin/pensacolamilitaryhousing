The five findings

  • Only 1 of 26 ZIPs pencils for an E-5 on Pensacola-side BAH. An FL064 E-5 with dependents draws $1,863 a month. The only ZIP where that covers the typical home's full ownership cost is 32505, west Pensacola, at $1,305 a month on a $144,691 typical value.
  • 12 of 26 ZIPs exceed every Pensacola-side grade. From Cantonment up through Gulf Breeze, the typical home's monthly cost is higher than the FL064 allowance of every grade on the table, O-6 included ($2,631).
  • The Eglin-side allowance is a different country. An FL023 E-5 ($2,433) covers the typical home in 12 of 26 ZIPs, and only Destin and Gulf Breeze proper beat every FL023 grade.
  • Same house, two answers. Milton's typical home costs $2,279 a month. On Pensacola-side rates that is E-9 territory. On Eglin-side rates every E-1 covers it. The duty-station rule, not the housing market, draws that line.
  • The median gap is real money. The median monthly ownership cost across all 26 ZIPs is $2,599. That is $736 above the FL064 E-5 rate and $166 above the FL023 E-5 rate.

How to read this honestly

Two things this table is not. It is not a "you cannot buy here" map: the typical value is Zillow's middle band for each ZIP, and real buyers routinely purchase below it, buy townhomes and condos, bring a second income, or assume a seller's low-rate VA loan, all of which beat the modeled number. And it is not a rent guide: BAH is calibrated to rents, and the renting-on-BAH picture looks considerably friendlier than this one.

What it is: an apples-to-apples answer to the question every PCSing family actually asks, which is whether the allowance covers the house everyone else seems to be buying. Both "Covered at" columns are shown for every ZIP because BAH follows your duty station, not your address: Milton is simultaneously an E-9 stretch for a NAS Pensacola sailor and an E-1 layup for a Hurlburt airman.

Methodology

  • Home values: Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI), all single-family homes and condos, 35th-65th percentile band, smoothed and seasonally adjusted, July 2026 vintage, by ZIP code, from Zillow's free public research data. 26 ZIPs across Escambia, Santa Rosa, and Okaloosa counties.
  • BAH: 2026 DoD-published with-dependents rates for MHA FL064 (Pensacola) and FL023 (Fort Walton Beach), per the DTMO lookup. Full tables on our BAH page.
  • Cost model (identical defaults to our BAH calculator): VA loan, zero down, 2.15% first-use funding fee financed into the loan, 6.5% rate, 30-year amortization, property tax at 1.0% of value per year, insurance at $3,000 per year. Monthly cost = principal and interest + tax + insurance.
  • Known simplifications: the flat $3,000 insurance figure understates waterfront and flood-zone ZIPs (Gulf Breeze, Navarre, Perdido, Destin can run well above it; see the insurance guide and flood-zone guide) and modestly overstates inland ones. The 1.0% tax rate is a pre-homestead planning figure. Rates move; at the time of publication 6.5% is the same planning assumption used across this site.
  • Refresh cadence: quarterly, and re-baselined each January when new BAH rates publish.

What it means if you are the one PCSing

If you are FL064 and E-6 or below, walk in knowing the typical home in most ZIPs is above your allowance alone: the workable plays are the west side and north Pensacola ZIPs at the top of the table, below-typical homes anywhere, the BAH-to-mortgage math run before you fall for a listing, or renting first on purpose. If you are FL023, your allowance is the strongest housing instrument on the Panhandle: Crestview and Milton price like the table's top half while your rates come from its bottom half, which is the whole thesis of the Crestview value play. Either way, the first move is the same 15 minutes it always is: rank, orders, report date, and the math comes back the same day.
