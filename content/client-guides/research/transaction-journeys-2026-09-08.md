# Transaction sequence revision: evidence and scope

Gregg requested complete transaction overviews, chronological explanations, financing-specific flowcharts and a detailed seller walkthrough/CMA/net sequence. The revision adds a dedicated investment guide and integrates the material into the full collection through `collection.mjs`. It preserves the original source blocks and the nine base contact directories.

Sources checked September 8: CFPB preapproval, loan options and Closing Disclosure; HUD Section 203(k); USDA guaranteed purchase eligibility; VA purchase process, Form 26-10291 and the eligibility FAQ; NAR pricing guide; IRS Publication 527 overview. Exact destinations and dates are in `journey-sources.mjs`. Original source dates remain September 6 and contact dates September 7. A VA Form 26-6381 landing-page request redirected to an old maintenance page; it was not used as evidence of current contact information or instructions.

The walkthrough, CMA presentation, preparation choices, net-input ownership and operating checklists are original Costin Team planning frameworks. No property address, MLS comparables, actual bids, lender quotes or property-specific fees were supplied; the publication does not claim to contain an actual CMA, appraisal, seller quote or investment recommendation.

New calculations use expressly hypothetical inputs. The sale waterfall is $450,000 minus $316,100, leaving $133,900. The three preparation scenarios yield $117,000, $122,000 and $120,000 after the shown costs. The rental example produces $21,600 NOI, $1,200 cash before tax after the separately shown debt service and reserve, 1.20 coverage under its stated definition and 1.2% cash-on-cash on an assumed $100,000 investment. Tax, insurance and debt-service definitions explicitly prevent escrow double counting. Vacancy and expense stress cases are scenarios, not likelihood estimates or market forecasts.

The investment cover is Pexels 29289011 by Chris F, selected after reviewing it and two rejected candidates. The Pexels license was checked this session and permits use without visible attribution. The source, license, image hash, visual decisions and crop are recorded internally in `covers.mjs` and `artifacts/client-library-design/journey-2026-09-08/cover-review.json`. The documents retain no visible photo credits. The illustration of a house is original vector line art, labeled conceptual and not a diagnosis or a representation of a particular property.

No publication, search-index submission, client message, performance claim or account change is part of this revision. Deliverables are the locally built PDFs, portable HTML, reviewed previews, staged PDF copies and gated archives. The agreed marketing and financing timelines remain transaction-specific.
