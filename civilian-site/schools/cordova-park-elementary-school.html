Get to know this school

A closer look at Cordova Park Elementary School.

Cordova Park Elementary is on Semur Road in Pensacola. Its published visual-arts curriculum includes making art, examining artworks and connecting artistic styles with history.

The 2025–26 Florida DOE report classifies Cordova Park Elementary School in the elementary category, as a district public school in Escambia County. Its accountability grade is B; it earned 61% of possible accountability points. The published grade sequence is A in 2023–24, A in 2024–25, and B in 2025–26.

An art program that describes thinking as well as making

Our interpretation of published school information, not a campus inspection or an additional official rating.

Cordova Park's published art information goes beyond a list of materials. It describes art history, production, criticism and aesthetics, with drawing, painting, collage, printmaking and three-dimensional work. Our reading is that the useful distinction is the combination of making something and learning to observe, describe and interpret it. Families can ask how that language develops as students move through the elementary grades.

The page also connects art with familiar places, imagination and interpretations of other artists' work. Those are useful starting points for a conversation about student choice and creative reasoning. They do not establish an arts-magnet program or a particular amount of weekly art instruction. We would examine how the specialist experience fits within the regular school week and whether families can see both finished work and the thinking behind it.

Why it may fit

Relevant for a family interested in how visual arts contribute to an elementary education. A child who enjoys making things may also benefit from learning to explain choices and respond thoughtfully to someone else's work.

What to weigh

A detailed subject description does not show current class frequency, materials available in each grade or how lessons are adapted. Confirm the present schedule and distinguish schoolwide instruction from any optional activity.

A useful local detail

The school is on Semur Road in Pensacola. Use the campus's current registration guidance to confirm the applicable grade placement and address assignment rather than relying on older directory entries.

One question worth asking

Ask to see a recent art project together with the learning goal, then ask how students discuss or revise their work and how art time is scheduled in your child's grade.

Understand the results

How the official results compare.

Its 61% overall points result is 3 percentage points above the 58% median among 33 other elementary schools in Escambia County with reported data.

What the results suggest.

Look beyond the latest letter

The sequence A → A → B is a reason to ask what changed between the three reporting years. A letter-grade change is not itself a measure of an individual child's learning growth. Ask the school to explain the underlying achievement and learning-gains results before attributing the change to a particular program.

A result worth noticing

Science is 74%, compared with a 61% median for 33 other local schools in the same state category. That 13-point difference is the largest positive peer gap among the subject measures available here. Ask which learning opportunities sit behind it and how a new student would access them.

The next question after the scores

None of the 3 subject measures with enough local comparison data falls below its own peer median. The next question is how the school supports both students who need more time and students ready for additional challenge. What should actually matter to parents

A letter grade is one data point, not a verdict. When families ask us how to evaluate a school, we point them to the same checklist every time:

  • Visit in person. Ten minutes in the front office and hallways tells you more about culture than any statistic.
  • Match the school to YOUR child. A school that is right for a self-directed kid may be wrong for one who needs structure, and the reverse. Programs (arts, STEM, athletics, IB/AP, ESE services) matter more than a one-letter summary.
  • Look at the trend, not one year. A school moving from C to B to A is a different story than one drifting the other way. We show three years on every school page for exactly this reason.
  • Read the components. Two schools with the same letter can have very different achievement profiles. The subject-area percentages show where a school is strong.
  • Confirm zoning before you buy. Attendance zones change. Verify the address you are considering with the district's current zoning tools, not a listing's claim.
  • Context matters. Research consistently shows family engagement outweighs most school-level differences. The percentages here measure the school; they do not predict your child.

Verify and dig deeper

  • Escambia County School District: enrollment, attendance zoning, school choice programs, and school contact pages.
  • Florida EduData portal: the state's full report card for every public school (search by school name).
  • All Pensacola-area school reports on this site, and the neighborhood guide for where each community sits.
