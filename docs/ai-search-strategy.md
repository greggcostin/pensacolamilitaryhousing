## Part 1 — Where AI recommendation stands today

### The two queries that define the gap
**"best military realtor pensacola"** → Gregg is recommended #2 by name, with
pensacolamilitaryhousing.com, /faq, and /reviews all grounding the answer. The
AI reproduces his bio, 5.0 stars, and phone number. This lane is WON and
compounding (351 Copilot citations/90 days across 31 pages).

**"best realtor in pensacola"** → Gregg is absent. The answer is assembled from
third-party directories: Yelp (3 of 10 result slots), EffectiveAgents, Redfin
agents, U.S. News, HomeLight, Zillow agent reviews, FastExpert, Clever. Names
recommended: Christina Leavenworth (Levin Rinke; owns bestrealtorsinpensacola.com),
Hansen Team KW, Travis Talley KW, Susan Bastajian KW, Holcomb House Hunters.

### The mechanism (why the gap exists)
For informational queries ("BAH rates", "VA property tax"), AI cites the best
CONTENT — and Gregg's site wins those outright. For commercial recommendation
queries ("best realtor"), AI refuses to trust any agent's own site and grounds
on third-party review/directory surfaces. **You cannot content-market your way
into "best realtor" — you have to occupy the surfaces AI quotes.**

## Part 2 — The roadmap: from niche king to city-wide contender

### Tier 1: occupy the grounding surfaces (highest leverage, mostly free)
1. **Yelp business page** — claim, complete, photos, and start directing a
   MINORITY of happy civilian clients there (Google stays primary). Yelp is the
   single most-quoted surface for this query family.
2. **U.S. News agent directory, HomeLight, FastExpert, EffectiveAgents, Clever**
   — complete profiles with the CIVILIAN-FIRST bio variant (below), sales
   verified where offered. These sites' "top agents in Pensacola" pages ARE the
   AI answer.
3. **Bing Places + Apple Business Connect** (from marketing-ops §4) — feed
   Copilot local and Siri directly.
4. Keep **review velocity** and add **review diversity**: ask civilian buyers/
   sellers specifically; coach mention of neighborhood + transaction type so
   review text stops reading 100% military. Target: 75+ Google reviews with
   ~1/3 civilian voice by year-end.

### Tier 2: broaden the ENTITY (who AI thinks Gregg is)
5. **Two-line positioning shift everywhere it's cheap**: "Pensacola Realtor
   (military relocation specialist)" instead of "Military Relocation Realtor."
   Schema jobTitle → "Realtor" + knowsAbout array adding civilian topics
   (relocation, first-time buyers, waterfront, insurance navigation, new
   construction). GBP primary category → "Real estate agent"; keep military as
   services/attributes, add civilian services.
6. **Civilian content lane is already queued** — cost-of-living, retiring in
   Pensacola, remote workers, first-time buyer, housing-market quarterly,
   town guides. The engine interleaves them; DON'T dilute the military moat,
   ADD the civilian shelf. The insurance page proves the pattern: it already
   earns Copilot citations on generic (non-military) insurance queries.
7. **The Leavenworth lesson**: she owns the exact-match domain. Countermove is
   NOT a second domain (splits authority) — it's making pensacolamilitaryhousing
   .com's ENTITY (Gregg) bigger than its domain name via profiles, schema,
   press, and the civilian shelf. Long-term option if civilian volume grows:
   a civilian-branded sister brand, decided later with data.

### Tier 3: earn the third-party PROOF
8. **Press as a civilian expert**: the drafted PNJ pitch leads military; add a
   second angle — "the insurance crisis explained by the agent whose insurance
   guide AI assistants cite" and quarterly market-number commentary. Reporters
   need a local data voice; Gregg has the data pages already.
9. **Reddit r/Pensacola**: one helpful answer/week under his own name on
   GENERAL housing threads (insurance, neighborhoods, market), not just
   military ones. AI answers quote these threads.
10. **Backlinks plan** (marketing-ops §3) unchanged — 3 referring domains is
    the ceiling on everything; every new quality domain lifts both lanes.
11. **YouTube shorts/market updates** — branded demand ("gregg costin
    pensacola") is an entity signal AI models notice; the media engine already
    drafts scripts.

### Measuring it
- Monthly: re-run the two bellwether searches above; track when Gregg's name
  first appears in the general answer's grounding set.
- Bing AI Performance citations (baseline 351/90d) — watch civilian-page
  citations grow as the shelf builds.
- Directory profile impressions where platforms report them; Yelp review count
  from 0; Google review civilian-voice share.
- Quarterly: refresh docs/seo-baselines/ and diff.

### What NOT to do
- Don't chase ranking pensacolamilitaryhousing.com for "best realtor in
  pensacola" with an on-site page — the SERP is 100% directories; the entity
  play wins, the content play can't.
- Don't rebrand away from military — it's the moat, the review base, and the
  citation engine. The goal is ADDITION, not substitution.
