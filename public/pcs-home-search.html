Search Smart: Three Rules for PCS Buyers

1. Budget from BAH, not from the top of your pre-approval. Lenders will approve you above your allowance. The BAH calculator shows the price where your full payment (principal, interest, taxes, and Florida insurance) stays inside the check that shows up on your LES.

2. Filter by commute gate, not city limits. "Pensacola" spans 30+ minutes of commute difference, and Eglin's East and West gates are different worlds. The base guides break down which neighborhoods feed which gate. Start there if you haven't picked a target area: NAS Pensacola, Whiting Field, Eglin AFB, Hurlburt Field.

3. Check the flood zone before you fall in love. Two identical houses a street apart can carry insurance quotes $200/month apart. The flood zone guide explains how to read the maps, and I screen every listing I send for exactly this.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to search?

No, the city links open live listings immediately with no registration wall. But if you're seriously shopping, set up the free RealScout account: you get instant MLS alerts, and I can see what you favorite, which makes every conversation we have sharper. It's the platform I run all my active clients on.

Are these all the listings, or a filtered subset?

It's the live Pensacola-area MLS feed, the same inventory every local agent sees, updated continuously. Zillow and Realtor.com pull from the same MLS but often lag on status changes, so a home that looks active there may already be pending here.

Why search here instead of Zillow?

Same homes, two differences: status accuracy (direct MLS feed, no lag) and where your inquiry goes. On the big portals, the agent who calls you back is whoever paid for your ZIP code. Here, your search connects to one MRP-certified military-relocation specialist: me.

Can you set up custom MLS alerts for me?

Yes, two ways. Fastest: create your own RealScout account (two minutes) and set your criteria; alerts start immediately. Or tell me base, budget, beds, and must-haves in the form on this page and I'll build the search for you. That's the best route when you're hunting from another duty station and want a human filtering the noise.

What about new construction and off-market homes?

Builder inventory doesn't always hit the MLS, and some sellers will take a strong offer without listing. I track both. See the new-construction guide, or just tell me what you need in the alert form and I'll include off-MLS options.

How do I know what my BAH actually buys?

Run the BAH calculator first. It converts your rank, dependent status, and MHA into a realistic zero-down VA purchase budget, then sends you back here with the searches pre-filtered to that number.

How is my buyer's agent paid on a VA loan?

Buyer-broker compensation is negotiable and put in writing before we tour a single home. In many Pensacola transactions the seller offers to cover some or all of it; where they do not, it becomes part of the offer strategy we build together. VA rules (VA Circular 26-24-14, as extended) allow a veteran to pay reasonable buyer-broker compensation when the seller does not. You will see every number up front, and nothing is signed until you understand it.
