The seller's tax bill can be a poor forecast of what you will pay after buying a Florida home. It may reflect years of capped assessments and exemptions that do not belong to you. Build your estimate from the parcel and your eligibility before using the monthly tax number in a mortgage quote.

Escambia and Santa Rosa follow Florida's assessment framework, but the taxing authorities and property circumstances vary by address. A Pensacola, Gulf Breeze or Milton mailing address does not by itself establish the city boundary or the applicable charges.

How are property taxes calculated in Escambia and Santa Rosa?

The basic ad valorem calculation is the applicable taxable value multiplied by each authority's millage, divided by 1,000. Then account for non-ad valorem assessments separately. School and non-school taxable values can differ, so one value multiplied by a combined rate can give the wrong answer.

Why can the tax bill increase after you buy?

A change of ownership generally brings reassessment to just value on January 1 after the purchase, with applicable exceptions. Florida's buyer guide explains why the prior owner's exemptions and assessment history cannot simply be carried into a buyer's next-year budget. Request an estimate for your ownership circumstances.

This is especially important when a listing displays a small tax bill for a home held for many years. The bill can be accurate history and still be a weak forecast. Ask the lender which tax estimate is being used and whether it reflects the expected reassessment.

  • Purchase year: Separate the current bill and contract proration from next year's assessment.
  • Following year: Estimate the property under the new ownership and approved benefits.
  • Later years: Revisit assessed value, adopted rates and additional charges instead of freezing the first estimate.

What is the Florida homestead exemption in 2026?

The 2026 additional homestead exemption is up to $26,411 for qualifying non-school taxable value, according to the Department of Revenue adjustment table. It is not a $26,411 refund and it does not reduce school taxes.

The first $25,000 of qualifying homestead exemption applies to all property-tax levies, including school taxes. Santa Rosa's exemption guidance explains that the additional tier applies to assessed value above $50,000, up to the applicable limit. Ownership, permanent residence and approval requirements still matter.

For a qualifying home with enough assessed value, those tiers can total $51,411 for non-school purposes while the school exemption remains $25,000. This is why subtracting the full amount from one combined taxable value overstates the savings. Older general brochures may still show an unadjusted second tier; the current-year adjustment controls the figure used here.

Does Save Our Homes cap the entire tax bill?

No. The 2026 Save Our Homes assessment cap is 2.7% for qualifying continuing homestead property, as reported by the Santa Rosa County Property Appraiser. It limits eligible assessed-value growth, not the full tax bill or the market value of the house.

Rate changes, additions and separate assessments can change the result. A buyer also needs to determine whether any portability benefit is available and approved rather than assuming the seller's accumulated benefit transfers with the sale. Give the appraiser the relevant prior-property history and ask for the calculation.

For a Pace buyer comparing an older owned home with new construction, request separate estimates for the completed properties. A bill reflecting vacant land or an unfinished improvement is not a forecast of taxes on the finished home. Our Pace neighborhood guide helps organize the other property questions.

What does a realistic tax example look like?

This hypothetical example assumes $350,000 assessed value, full qualifying 2026 homestead, seven school mills, ten non-school mills and a $300 separate assessment. The millages and assessment are invented to explain the method; they are not the adopted rates for an actual address.

  • School tax: $325,000 taxable value times 0.007 equals $2,275.
  • Non-school tax: $298,589 taxable value times 0.010 equals $2,985.89.
  • Illustrated annual total: $2,275 plus $2,985.89 plus $300 equals $5,560.89.
  • Monthly budget equivalent: $5,560.89 divided by 12 equals $463.41 after rounding.

The monthly equivalent is a planning amount, not the lender's escrow calculation. Reproduce the example with the correct taxable values and adopted levies for the property. If an exemption is uncertain, keep a second budget without it until the appraiser resolves eligibility.

Which deadlines should you check now?

Escambia's published 2026 Value Adjustment Board petition deadline was September 8. As of this September 9 review, that date has passed. The appraiser's current calendar is the source; contact the appraiser or VAB promptly about any remaining procedure instead of assuming an extension.

For Santa Rosa, use your notice and the county's current instructions. Do not borrow Escambia's date just because both counties use TRIM notices. A value question, an exemption question and a proposed millage question can also have different responsible offices.

  1. Find the parcel number and the notice's tax year.
  2. Check the assessed values, exemption codes and listed authorities.
  3. Identify whether the concern is value, eligibility, rate or a separate assessment.
  4. Contact the responsible office and document the answer and deadline.
  5. Keep copies of any submission and its acknowledgement.

What changes for rentals, second homes and coastal parcels?

Budget the property for its actual use and jurisdiction. Do not assume a homestead benefit applies to a vacation home or investment property. A rental projection that inherits the seller's exempted bill can overstate the cash available after expenses.

For a Gulf Breeze property, confirm the parcel's city or unincorporated location before selecting tax rates. Then add insurance and any association charges to the ownership-cost comparison. The Gulf Breeze guide provides a place to organize those checks.

  • Buyer: Obtain a next-year estimate and preserve a cushion for changes.
  • Seller: Provide the current bill with its tax year and avoid promising the buyer the same payment.
  • Investor: Carry full supportable taxes in the operating budget until benefits are confirmed.
